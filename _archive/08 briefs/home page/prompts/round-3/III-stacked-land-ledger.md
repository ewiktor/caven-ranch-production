# III — stacked land ledger

Build a 1440 by 1100 desktop homepage opening for Caven Ranch. This is a real first screen plus the beginning of the first scroll for a planned hospitality site, not a generic hotel landing page. The design must make the land the reason to stay: Caven Ranch is 1,000 acres on the Pedernales River, held by the same family for more than 150 years, and becoming a small, design-led retreat outside Johnson City. The design should feel like a strong editorial web composition with real photographic proof, not a blank white brochure.

Use the Soft Blue color direction only: off white `#ECE7E3` as the primary ground, deep black `#000A0A` as every type colour, soft blue `#D6E0E2` as the single large colour moment, and sage gray `#919D9D` only for an image-mat edge. Use Piazzolla 500 at 56px for display type and Familjen Grotesk 500 at 13px and 18px for utility and body text. Use mixed case only. Never use uppercase, coloured type, green, paper texture, drop shadows, labels that pretend to be technical data, brackets, decorative numbers, or text placed on photography.

Build a compact 72px top header on off white with `Caven Ranch` left and `the land`, `lodgings`, `stories`, and `marsha's list` right. Directly below, create a 220px soft-blue title band with a small near-black label `Texas Hill Country, held in one family` and the display sentence `The land comes first here.` The title band is not mostly empty. It sets up the image below with a deliberate lower-left text block and substantial open space to its right.

Under that band, use a wide 1040px by 620px framed Caven aerial image, set slightly left of center with a 1px sage-gray outline and no shadow. Use `https://media.withdraper.com/1bc4729a-07cd-4572-98aa-8402c8bb5ea8/254e1a9220445fbdeac066ef72ee1b03a137342b4f24373beb0d9c76f646f796` and crop it to preserve the river's diagonal path. Beneath the image, on off white, place the small supporting copy `A thousand acres along the Pedernales River are becoming a small, design-led retreat outside Johnson City.` Then make `Caven Ranch` a large near-black Piazzolla wordmark-like display line beneath the supporting copy, so the page has a clear vertical image, small-text, large-name rhythm.

Interrupt the lower-right edge of the aerial frame with one smaller, bordered horizontal inset of the limestone riverbed image, using `https://media.withdraper.com/1bc4729a-07cd-4572-98aa-8402c8bb5ea8/3347d2354c58a082fd1f6dc8581d9db4a0c78f41ba4bf4e64449be771cea3a0f`. The inset must partly obscure the aerial and partly sit on the off-white ground. This is a picture-in-picture reading device that turns the property-scale photograph into a close physical experience. Do not add a third image, an illustration, a caption, or extra copy.

Use `https://media.withdraper.com/internal-references/208648df-e63f-402c-9d4b-6739a4533cd1/original.jpg` for the vertical image, small-text, large-name hierarchy and `https://media.withdraper.com/1bc4729a-07cd-4572-98aa-8402c8bb5ea8/3e2f8b2ae7cd53378372ccbffc9e80b9027030c866c87797fa10e9648a93f807` for the inset interruption principle. These are structural references only, not assets to display. The resulting page should be bolder than Round 2 because the hierarchy, crop, and inset create tension, while still being calm and usable.
