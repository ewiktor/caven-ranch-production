# II — edges only · Marcellus + topo ground

Weight at the margins, middle empty. Display face: Marcellus (warm classical serif). Carries the topo-on-cream ground from The Acres reference. Positive spec only.

```
Design a desktop web page: Marsha's List, for Caven Ranch. Frame 1440 x 1000px. A single static layout.

THE IDEA
Everything is pushed out to the edges of the frame and the middle is left almost entirely open. A small block of type floats in the centre of that space. The photographs cling to the margins, several of them running off the frame. The openness is the subject: this is a page about land that is being left alone.

GROUND
Warm bone #F6F3EE across the full frame, carrying the property's own contour lines as a soft ground texture: use
https://media.withdraper.com/1bc4729a-07cd-4572-98aa-8402c8bb5ea8/8255ba92b312e5d921b0b16d08092e169b96e66c8bfbb429671e7ce848b5f0ca
scaled to cover the frame and bleeding off all four edges, fully desaturated, its linework tinted toward warm taupe #C7C0B2 and held at 8 percent opacity. It should read as a faint watermark in the paper, felt at the edge of vision. Keep it well under the type and the images.

THE CENTRE — a small type block at x=720 y=500, centred
- "Marsha's List" in Marcellus, 46px, warm near-black #241E1A.
- Below it, 24px gap: "a record of what lives here, kept because most of this land is staying exactly as it is" in Marcellus 17px, centred, max-width 340px, line-height 1.55.
- Keep it small and quiet. The open space around it should read as far larger than the type itself.

THE MARGINS — seven images, all in the outer band, none closer than 300px to the centre point
Four of the seven run off the frame edge, showing 40 to 70 percent of themselves. Scales run 120px to 380px wide. Cluster two near the top-left corner and two near the bottom-right so the frame is weighted on a diagonal, with the other three spread loosely. Give each a 1px #C7C0B2 hairline at 40 percent opacity.

TYPE
Marcellus throughout, in sentence case. Set in warm near-black #241E1A.

COLOUR
Ground warm bone #F6F3EE. Ground contours warm taupe #C7C0B2 at 8 percent. Type warm near-black #241E1A. Hairlines #C7C0B2. The photographs carry all the colour in the frame.

SPACING
Use 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96 / 128 / 160.
```
