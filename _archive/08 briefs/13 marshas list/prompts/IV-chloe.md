Design a desktop web page: Marsha's List, for Caven Ranch. Frame 1440 x 1190px. A single static layout.

THE IDEA
An almost empty warm-cream page. A thin nav strip across the very top with the name centred in it, then a vast open field. One centred serif sentence sits in the middle of that emptiness with a small illustration directly beneath it. Exactly four images sit far out at the edges and corners — upper-left, mid-left, mid-right, lower-right — none of them anywhere near the centre. The emptiness is most of the page and it is the entire point: a quiet index of living things that does not crowd you.

GROUND
Warm cream #EBE8E0 across the full frame, with a very fine paper grain at 4 percent opacity. Felt, not seen.

THE NAV STRIP — full width, 96px tall, flush at the very top
- Its own slightly lighter ground, #F2F0EA, meeting the page cream on a clean horizontal edge, no rule or border.
- Centred: "Caven Ranch" in Playfair Display, 26px, letter-spacing 0.06em, near-black #1C1815, mixed case.
- Left group starting at x=96, Playfair Display 15px, near-black, 44px between items: "Lodgings" · "Experiences" · "Stories"
- Right group ending at x=1344, same style, 44px between items: "Marsha's List" · "Social" · "Book"
- All sentence case, quiet. The nav reads as a caption, not a header.

THE CENTRE — a single centred block at x=720, first baseline at y=555
Three centred lines in Playfair Display, 34px, line-height 1.55, near-black #1C1815, roman with the named things in true italic:
  line 1: A record of the *little bluestem*,
  line 2: *the Pedernales*, & the animals
  line 3: that were here first
(The words in asterisks are italic — a real italic cut, not a slant. The italic ampersand is the nicest character on the page.)

THE MARK — centred at x=720, top edge at y=735
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewiktor/caven-ranch-production/main/05%20graphics/assets/paper-run-02/little-bluestem.png
(little bluestem grass, painted, transparent background). Render it 110px tall, alone in the open space with nothing near it. It is a punctuation mark, not an image.
