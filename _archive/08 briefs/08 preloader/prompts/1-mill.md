Design a website preloader for Caven Ranch as a single static frame. 1440 x 900px. One still image, no animation.

REFERENCE — study this before you place anything:
https://media.withdraper.com/1bc4729a-07cd-4572-98aa-8402c8bb5ea8/7a04d6aea700399c8e4cb98619d4e7bdac974cb1efd82458d91ceac7ff776622
What to bring forward from it: the entire typographic block crushed into the bottom-left corner, leaving a vast empty butter-cream field taking up roughly three quarters of the frame. The way the fat script word sits INSIDE the serif sentence, sharing its baseline, so the sentence reads straight through the script and out the other side — it is one continuous line of reading, not a logo with a caption. The tightness of the paragraph: three lines, set solid, jammed hard into the corner with a small even margin. The confidence of leaving that much of the page empty.
What NOT to take from it: its typeface, and its cropping.

THE FRAME
Ground: butter cream #F3EAD0 across the full 1440 x 900, with a fine even paper grain at 5 percent opacity. It should feel like a warm sheet of stock.

BOTTOM-LEFT — the type block, starting x=72, last baseline at y=836
Three lines, set solid at line-height 1.15, Petrona 34px weight 500, near-black #1F1B16, ragged right, max-width 640px:
  "Caven Ranch is 1,000 acres on the Pedernales, kept by one family for more than 150 years."
The word "Caven" at the very start is set in Yellowtail at 60px, sitting ON the same baseline as the rest of line one, its descender free to hang below. The Petrona sentence continues immediately after it with a normal word space. Script and serif touch and share the line. That join is the whole design.

BOTTOM-RIGHT — the coyote, lying down
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewiktor/caven-ranch-production/main/05%20graphics/assets/paper-run-02/coyotes/v1-coyote-resting-right-dither.png
A resting coyote rendered as a fine print halftone, transparent background, facing right. Place it 300px tall with its right edge at x=1368 and its base at y=850, so it sits low in the corner, roughly level with the type block and looking out of the frame. It is a quiet, deadpan presence in the opposite corner from the words.

THE VOID
Everything above y=600 stays completely empty butter cream. That emptiness is the design and it is placed on purpose.

TYPE
Petrona for the sentence, sentence case. Yellowtail for the word "Caven" only. Near-black #1F1B16.

COLOUR
Ground butter cream #F3EAD0. Type and halftone near-black #1F1B16. Nothing else.
