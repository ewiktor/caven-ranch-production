Design a website preloader for Caven Ranch as a single static frame. 1440 x 900px. One still image, no animation.

REFERENCE 1 — the print-proof register:
https://media.withdraper.com/1bc4729a-07cd-4572-98aa-8402c8bb5ea8/778956d1eef839a65bccc4fcd30bdfcb714d2175aab5a1cd09b33c9e9ae6cf3f
What to bring forward from it: sequential frames of the same animal layered at rising opacities so that time reads as density — the faintest frame is the oldest, the darkest is now. The halftone dither that makes it read as something printed rather than rendered. The idea that motion can be shown in one still frame by stacking its moments.
What NOT to take: its crop marks, its registration ticks, its ghosted repeated headline, and its aged paper. Those read as costume. The dither and the layered time are what survive.

REFERENCE 2 — the sequence itself, and the exact register to match:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewiktor/caven-ranch-production/main/05%20graphics/assets/paper-run-01/08-walkin-sequence-v1.png
This is Caven's own round-one artwork: five coyotes in a horizontal sequence, walking in from the left and settling into a lie-down at the right, each frame denser than the last, all in a fine halftone on cream. This is the image the frame is built around. Use it as-is at full width. It already carries the idea; the job here is to give it a designed page instead of leaving it floating.

THE FRAME
Ground: warm cream #EFEBE2 across the full 1440 x 900, with a fine even paper grain at 5 percent opacity.

THE SEQUENCE — the hero, placed high and wide
Place the sequence image 1180px wide, centred horizontally at x=720, with its base at y=560. It bleeds slightly past nothing and sits cleanly in the upper two thirds. Its own cream matches the page cream, so it should read as printed directly onto the sheet with no edge, no frame, no hairline, no shadow. If its background is not perfectly matching, multiply it into the page so the white disappears and only the halftone animal remains.

BOTTOM-LEFT — the number
"64" in Prata, 200px, near-black #1C1813, left edge at x=64, baseline at y=852. It is enormous and it is the second loudest thing on the page after the coyotes. No percent sign, no label, no bar, no track, nothing beside it. Just the number, big, sitting in the corner.

BOTTOM-RIGHT — one small line, right-aligned, ending at x=1376, baseline y=846
"Caven Ranch" in Prata 15px, near-black #1C1813 at 70 percent opacity. Quiet. It is a signature, not a header.

THE VOID
The band between the base of the sequence and the top of the number, roughly y=560 to y=690, stays empty cream. The number rises out of that emptiness.

TYPE
Prata throughout, sentence case, near-black #1C1813.

COLOUR
Ground warm cream #EFEBE2. Halftone and type near-black #1C1813. Two colours on the page.
