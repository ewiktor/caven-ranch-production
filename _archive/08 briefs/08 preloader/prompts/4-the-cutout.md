Design a website preloader for Caven Ranch as a single static frame. 1440 x 900px. One still image, no animation.

REFERENCE — study this before you place anything:
https://media.withdraper.com/1bc4729a-07cd-4572-98aa-8402c8bb5ea8/3e2f8b2ae7cd53378372ccbffc9e80b9027030c866c87797fa10e9648a93f807
What to bring forward from it: a huge flat black animal silhouette, cropped hard by the frame, reading as pure shape with no interior detail at all. And then a rectangular window punched into the middle of that shape, which reveals the same animal again in full detail and in a different tone entirely — so the eye gets shape, then suddenly gets a face. The window has sharp geometric edges and does not align to anything about the animal; it simply cuts. The jump between flat black and detailed rendering inside the window is violent, and that violence is the idea. It is one animal shown twice at two different truths, in one frame.
The reference does this with a Doberman. Caven does it with the coyote.

THE FRAME
Ground: warm cream #F2EFE8 across the full 1440 x 900, with a fine paper grain at 4 percent opacity.

THE SILHOUETTE — the big shape
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewiktor/caven-ranch-production/main/05%20graphics/assets/paper-run-02/coyotes/v1-coyote-resting-right-silhouette.png
A resting coyote as a solid flat shape with no interior detail, transparent background, facing right. Render it very large: 1150px wide, its left edge at x=90, its base at y=800. Fill it with deep walnut #2A1D14. It should dominate the frame and read as a single confident shape.

THE WINDOW — the cut
Punch a rectangle 340 x 380px into the silhouette, positioned over the coyote's head and muzzle, roughly at x=790 y=250. Give it hard square corners and no border, no outline, no shadow.
Inside that window, place:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewiktor/caven-ranch-production/main/05%20graphics/assets/paper-run-02/coyotes/v1-coyote-head-detail.png
This is the same coyote's head and nose, painted in full detail. Scale and position it inside the window so that the head sits at the same angle and roughly the same place it would occupy in the silhouette behind it, but noticeably larger, about 1.4 times the scale — so it lines up conceptually and refuses to line up exactly. The eye, the muzzle and the nose must all be inside the window; the nose sits near the window's right edge.
Fill the window's ground, behind and around the head, with deep walnut #2A1D14, darker than the page and matching the silhouette. The painted head sits on that dark ground. So: cream page, walnut shape, and inside the cut another walnut field carrying a fully rendered face.
The window's edges cut straight through the silhouette. Where the window overlaps the coyote's outline, the window wins and the silhouette simply stops.

BOTTOM-LEFT — one small line, x=72, baseline y=852
"Caven Ranch" in Familjen Grotesk 14px, weight 500, deep walnut #2A1D14. That is the only text on the page.

TYPE
Familjen Grotesk, sentence case, weight 500.

COLOUR
Ground warm cream #F2EFE8. Silhouette and window field deep walnut #2A1D14. The painted head inside the window carries its own warm greys and tans, and it is the only place on the page where colour appears. That is the reward for looking into the cut.
