Design a desktop web section: the property map for Caven Ranch. Frame 1440 x 1000px. A single static layout.

THE IDEA
The ranch drawn from memory by someone who walks it every day. A full-bleed hand-sketched map in thin warm-black ink on cream: the river as two loose parallel lines swinging across the lower third, the ridge as a handful of short hatch strokes, clusters of small circles for the oak stands, a dotted line for the track down to the water. Most of the page stays empty cream. The charm is in the little handwritten notes pinned to real places. It reads like a ranger's note left on a kitchen table, drawn with confidence and left airy.

GEOGRAPHY — draw it from this real aerial, loosely, not traced:
https://media.withdraper.com/1bc4729a-07cd-4572-98aa-8402c8bb5ea8/5141ae7ee7491e1bd766c739a8fc84d6de0f5cb8f56b6b2031e17f653c63e11f
(the property from above: the riverbed swinging along one edge, scrubland, elevation changes). Keep the true shape of the river bend and where the land rises. Everything is simplified into a few confident thin strokes.

GROUND
Warm white #F7F3F0 across the full frame, fine paper grain at 4 percent opacity.

THE INK
All linework in a single warm black #2A241E, thin and even, 1 to 1.5px optical weight, drawn slightly imperfect: lines that wobble a little, circles that do not quite close. The river's two lines are the longest, loosest strokes on the page. No shading, no fills, no colour inside the map. The drawing occupies the full frame but at maybe 30 percent ink density: the cream does most of the work.

THE NOTES — small handwritten-register labels in Special Elite, 13px, warm black #2A241E, each sitting beside its true place with a short thin arrow or a small x marking the spot:
  "turkeys, usually" by the oak cluster upper-right
  "swim here when it is warm" at the wide pool of the river bend
  "the crossing goes under after rain" at the low-water crossing
  "cabins will sit up here, out of sight of each other" on the ridge
  "coyote prints most mornings" near the track, lower-left
Five notes, no more. Each is one line. They are observations, not marketing.

THE TITLE — bottom-left, x=88, baseline y=900
"the land, roughly" in Piazzolla italic, 30px, warm black #2A241E. Under it at 12px gap, Piazzolla roman 14px: "drawn from the north aerial, not to scale".

THE COMPASS — a lone "N" in Special Elite 13px with a short thin hand-drawn arrow above it, top-right, slightly crooked on purpose.

TYPE
Special Elite for the notes and the N. Piazzolla for the title block. Sentence case everywhere.

COLOUR
Cream #F7F3F0 and warm black #2A241E. Two colours on the page, nothing else.
