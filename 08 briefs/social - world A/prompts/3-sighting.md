# World A · post 3 — character sighting

V1 · 2026-07-17 · Concept: [social - character sightings](../../../07%20content%20concepts/social%20-%20character%20sightings.md). Same type and colour system as posts 1 and 2, but the format is the presence rule: nothing is explained, so the type nearly disappears. This post is the stress test — if the system still reads as the same brand with almost no type on it, the system is real.

```
Design a social post, 1080 x 1350 (4:5). A single static layout.

THE IDEA
Two coyotes standing in dry grass at dusk, looking back at you. Nothing is said about them. No caption, no name, no explanation, no arrow pointing them out. You either notice them or you don't, and the post trusts you to. Almost the whole frame is the photograph, and the only type is the brand signing the bottom quietly.

GROUND
Off white (#ECE7E3) across the full frame.

THE PHOTOGRAPH — x=72 to x=1008, y=72 to y=1178
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewiktor/caven-ranch-production/main/05%20graphics/assets/paper-run-02/coyote-sighting.png
(two coyotes standing in tall dry gold grass at dusk, live oak and juniper around them, limestone boulders and Hill Country ridgeline behind, soft overcast light). Render 936 x 1106px with object-fit cover. Keep the coyotes at their natural size in the frame, small and easy to miss, roughly centred and slightly low. Give it a 1px border in walnut (#432F2E) at 25 percent.

THE FOOT — left, x=72, baseline about y=1250
"Caven Ranch" in Hanken Grotesk at 18px, mixed case, walnut (#432F2E).

That is the entire post. Nothing else appears on it.

TYPE
Hanken Grotesk, once, small. Mixed case.

COLOUR
Off white ground (#ECE7E3). The foot in walnut (#432F2E). The photograph carries everything else. No rust on this one.

SPACING
An even 72px margin on the left, right, and top, and a deeper margin at the bottom where the foot sits. The photograph is the post.
```
