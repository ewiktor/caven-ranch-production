# World A · post 2 — heritage fragment: the shell

V1 · 2026-07-17 · Concept: [social - heritage fragments](../../../07%20content%20concepts/social%20-%20heritage%20fragments.md). Same type and colour system as posts 1 and 3. Round-2 kills honoured: no numbering, no object-spec caption, the image gets a border rather than full bleed, and Special Elite stays small.

```
Design a social post, 1080 x 1350 (4:5). A single static layout.

THE IDEA
One object, and the plain story of how it got here. The shell is warm brass against a warm ground, so the whole frame sits close in temperature and the engraved figures are the only sharp thing in it.

GROUND
Off white (#ECE7E3) across the full frame.

THE LABEL — top left, x=72, baseline about y=110
"Still at the ranch" in Special Elite at 18px, mixed case, walnut (#432F2E).

THE OBJECT — centred, x=190 to x=890, y=170 to y=830
https://media.withdraper.com/1bc4729a-07cd-4572-98aa-8402c8bb5ea8/60899228b6f2ee157aeab16360070575f23bd9482a972569c434c876dbf5596b
(a spent artillery shell in patinated dark brass, hand-engraved with a scene of cavalry riders in fine incised line). Render 700 x 660px with object-fit cover, cropped close so the engraved riders read clearly. Give it a 1px border in walnut (#432F2E) at 25 percent, and a soft shadow beneath it: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,10,10,0.10) and 0 14px 30px rgba(0,10,10,0.10).

THE LINE — left, x=72, width 860px, first baseline about y=940
Three lines in Prata at 40px, line-height 56px, mixed case, deep black (#000A0A):
"Before he left France, Ralph had a French artist
engrave his spent shell with cavalry riders.
He brought it home."

THE RULE — left, x=72, y=1140, a 40px horizontal rule, 2px, rust (#813517)
The only rust on the page.

THE NOTE — left, x=72, width 720px, baseline about y=1190
One line in Hanken Grotesk at 22px, mixed case, deep black (#000A0A):
"He was in the last cavalry unit the army had."

THE FOOT — left, x=72, baseline about y=1270
"Caven Ranch" in Hanken Grotesk at 18px, mixed case, walnut (#432F2E).

TYPE
Prata for the story lines. Hanken Grotesk for the note and the foot. Special Elite for the small top label only, never large. Mixed case throughout.

COLOUR
Off white ground (#ECE7E3). Type deep black (#000A0A), chrome walnut (#432F2E). Rust (#813517) once, as the rule. The brass carries the rest.

SPACING
The object sits high and the words sit under it with room. Use 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96.
```
