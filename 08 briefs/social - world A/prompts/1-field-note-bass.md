# World A · post 1 — field note: Guadalupe bass

V1 · 2026-07-17 · Concept: [social - marshas field notes](../../../07%20content%20concepts/social%20-%20marshas%20field%20notes.md). One of three posts sharing a single type and colour system, so the system can be judged across three unlike subjects. World A = editorial serif (Prata) + grotesk chrome (Hanken Grotesk), Direction 2 (Rust).

```
Design a social post, 1080 x 1350 (4:5). A single static layout.

THE IDEA
A catalogue entry for one fish. The ink-on-fabric print of the bass does all the looking; the type names it and places it, and gets out of the way. Quiet, warm, and made rather than posted.

GROUND
Off white (#ECE7E3) across the full frame.

THE LABEL — top left, x=72, baseline about y=110
"Marsha's list" in Hanken Grotesk at 20px, lowercase, letter-spacing 0.2em, walnut (#432F2E).

THE SPECIMEN — centred, x=140 to x=940, y=180 to y=820
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewiktor/caven-ranch-production/main/05%20graphics/assets/marshas-list/gyotaku-guadalupe-bass-rust-v1.png
(a Guadalupe bass pressed in rust ink into woven cream fabric, the fish bisected so its back half runs along the top edge and its front half along the bottom, its name stamped small and imperfect on the cloth). Render 800 x 640px with object-fit cover. Give it a 1px border in walnut (#432F2E) at 25 percent. It carries its own stamped label, so add nothing on top of it.

THE NAME — left, x=72, baseline about y=930
"Guadalupe bass" in Prata at 62px, mixed case, deep black (#000A0A), tracking slightly tightened.

THE RULE — left, x=72, y=970, a 40px horizontal rule, 2px, rust (#813517)
This is the only rust on the page outside the print itself.

THE NOTE — left, x=72, width 720px, first baseline about y=1030
Two lines in Hanken Grotesk at 24px, line-height 36px, mixed case, deep black (#000A0A):
"The state fish of Texas. It lives in our stretch of the Pedernales, and it wears that striping down its side."

THE FOOT — left, x=72, baseline about y=1270
"Caven Ranch" in Hanken Grotesk at 18px, mixed case, walnut (#432F2E).

TYPE
Prata for the name. Hanken Grotesk for the label, the note, and the foot. Mixed case and lowercase throughout.

COLOUR
Off white ground (#ECE7E3). Type deep black (#000A0A), chrome walnut (#432F2E). Rust (#813517) once, as the rule. The print carries the rest.

SPACING
Keep the bottom third open and let the type sit low and left. Use 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96.
```
