# Home page — round 3 layout study

## Editorial outline

The first screen must make Caven Ranch legible as a place before it tries to make it aspirational: a 1,000-acre family ranch on the Pedernales River, held by the same family for more than 150 years, becoming a small design-led retreat. The order is land and scale first, then the accommodation as a means of experiencing the land. The feeling is a quiet jolt of discovery, not generic hospitality calm.

The available visual proof is the aerial river meander, the cabins held inside oak cover, the low-angle arrival view, and the limestone riverbed. No booking or operating claim is made.

## Visual direction

This set tests three different hero systems, not three reskins: a media portal with a paging rhythm, a color-and-image split unified by one crossing graphic, and a vertical editorial stack interrupted by an inset landscape crop. Each starts from a real layout reference and uses Caven media as content. The common correction is to avoid Round 1's text placed over a hero photograph and Round 2's detached text on a pale field.

Piazzolla is tested as the leading display candidate, paired with Familjen Grotesk for utility text. Type remains near-black on light fields. The palette direction rotates across the three studies. There are no uppercase labels, paper grounds, technical brackets, drop shadows, fabricated data, or decorative numbering.
