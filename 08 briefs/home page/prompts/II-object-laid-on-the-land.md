# II — the object laid on the land

Home page. Structural axis: no split at all — one full-bleed aerial, with a physical object laid on top of it and vertical type following the object's axis. Color direction 4a (Slate).

```
Build a desktop home page for Caven Ranch. Frame: 1440 x 1800px.

THE ONE IDEA
One full-bleed aerial photograph of the land, with a physical object lying on top of it as though someone set it down on the image — casting a real contact shadow, sitting at a slight angle off-grid. The brand name and information run vertically, rotated 90 degrees counter-clockwise, following the object's vertical axis rather than the page grid. The page reads as a surface with something resting on it, not as a layout.

STRUCTURE
- Full-bleed photograph, edge to edge, the entire 1440 x 1800 frame: https://media.withdraper.com/1bc4729a-07cd-4572-98aa-8402c8bb5ea8/254e1a9220445fbdeac066ef72ee1b03a137342b4f24373beb0d9c76f646f796 (high-angle aerial, winding river as a diagonal leading line, horizon in the upper third).
- Apply a soft vertical scrim over the photograph so type stays legible: Warm White #F7F3F0 at 0 percent opacity at the top, rising to 45 percent opacity at the bottom edge. No hard band, no rectangle.
- THE OBJECT: a tall vertical card, 300px wide by 1080px tall, placed at x=190, y=380, rotated -3 degrees so it sits deliberately off the page grid. Ground Warm White #F7F3F0. It carries a soft, believable contact shadow: 40px blur, 20 percent opacity, offset 8px down and 4px right, so it reads as a physical thing resting on the photograph rather than a UI panel. Square corners, no border radius, no outline.
- Inside the object, top: the word "Caven" in the script face, 56px, ink. Below it at 24px gap: the body paragraph, Geist 16px, max-width 220px. Bottom of the object: the small line "outside johnson city, texas" in Geist 13px.
- THE VERTICAL TYPE: the headline is rotated 90 degrees counter-clockwise and runs UP the right side of the frame, x=1290, baseline aligned so the text block runs from y=1500 up to y=520. It is set in Piazzolla 64px. It must overlap the aerial photograph directly and must NOT sit inside any panel or box of its own — it lies on the land.
- The object's right edge (x=490) and the vertical headline (x=1290) leave the centre of the frame completely open: the river runs through that void untouched. The void is the composition. Do not fill it.
- Small wordmark "caven ranch" top-right, x=1296, y=64, Geist 400, 13px, horizontal, near-white #F7F3F0.

TYPE
- Display: Piazzolla, weight 500, tracking -0.02em, line-height 1.05.
- Script accent, for the word "Caven" ONLY: Yellowtail, 56px.
- Utility/body: Geist, weight 400.
- Headline (vertical): Piazzolla 64px, two lines.
- Body: Geist 16px, line-height 1.55.
- Type is near-black ink #111111 only, except the top-right wordmark which is near-white over the photograph (that is scrim contrast, not coloured type). Never colour type with an accent. Never green.

COPY (use exactly, no changes)
Vertical headline (runs up the right side): "The river has been in this family for more than 150 years."
Script word on the object: "Caven"
Body on the object: "One thousand acres in the Texas Hill Country, on a bend of the Pedernales. The same family has looked after it for more than 150 years, and most of it is now held in conservation. A small retreat is being built into one corner, and the rest is staying exactly as it is."
Small line at the bottom of the object: "outside johnson city, texas"
Wordmark: "caven ranch"

COLOUR — direction 4a, Slate. Total colours on screen: 5 maximum.
- Ground: Warm White #F7F3F0
- Ink: Coal Black #111111
- Accents: Slate Blue #99A2AD, Saddle #B68861, Olive #969581
- Use at most ONE of the three accents, as a single small moment — the aerial photograph is doing the colour work. Five weak accents equals no accent.

SPACING — use only these values: 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96 / 128 / 160.
Within-group gaps must be at most one third of between-group gaps. Make the ratio sharp: 8 inside a group, 64 between groups.

DO NOT
- Do not let the vertical headline sit inside a panel, card or box. It lies directly on the photograph.
- Do not straighten the object to the grid. The -3 degree rotation is the point.
- Do not use uppercase anywhere, in any element, at any size.
- Do not use em dashes in any copy.
- Do not put a caption or explainer line under the image.
- Do not add decorative chrome: no corner numbers, no plate numbers, no fake-technical data labels, no bracket framing, no small accent rules or ticks above labels.
- Do not add map pins, location tags, or region labels to the aerial.
- Do not use green in any form.
- Do not make the object look like aged paper, a scrapbook page, a torn note or an old document. It is a clean contemporary card that happens to be a physical object.
- Do not state or imply that the retreat is open, bookable, or operating. It is in development. No prices, no booking button, no "reserve".
- Do not use Fraunces, Space Grotesk, Archivo Black, Instrument Serif, Geist as a display face, or any mono font.
```
