# II — crossing split

Build a 1440 by 960 desktop homepage hero for Caven Ranch. This is a real first screen for a planned hospitality site, not a generic hotel landing page. The design must communicate that Caven Ranch is 1,000 acres of family-held Hill Country land on the Pedernales River and is becoming a small, design-led retreat. It needs an unmistakable physical layout move that gives the page depth and personality without reverting to the bad type, arbitrary colour, or technical decoration of Round 1.

Use the Slate color direction only: warm white `#F7F3F0`, near-black `#111111` for every piece of type, slate blue `#99A2AD` as the single structural colour field, and saddle `#B68861` only as a small non-typographic graphic accent. Use Piazzolla 500 at 64px for the display headline and Familjen Grotesk 500 at 13px and 18px for the UI and body. Use mixed case only. Never use uppercase, coloured type, green, paper texture, drop shadows, fabricated technical data, corner numbering, text directly over the photo, or more than one illustration.

Make the screen a weighted 44/56 split. The left warm-white panel carries a 72px header with `Caven Ranch` and the navigation `the land`, `lodgings`, `stories`, and `marsha's list`. Its main content is a large left-aligned headline, `A thousand acres where the river still sets the terms.`, with the smaller supporting copy, `Caven Ranch is becoming a small, design-led retreat on family land outside Johnson City.` Set both in near-black. A short low-left detail line reads `1,000 acres · Pedernales River · more than 150 years`, with normal sentence case and no fake labels. Leave at least a third of the left panel open.

The right 56 percent is one full-height Caven image only, using the oak-framed cabin image `https://media.withdraper.com/1bc4729a-07cd-4572-98aa-8402c8bb5ea8/3afdd839f49e8c95ce003a213a5e9c53734b40612cbcb85b8a2257defa55e002`. Let the photo bleed to the outer edge, but place no words directly on it. At the seam, add one small slate-blue information card overlapping 96px into the photo. The card contains only `the first view arrives at the cabin` and `the living space looks west through glass`, both in near-black, plus a single small saddle square. It is an editorial card with a 1px low-opacity outline, not a floating product card and not a shadowed component.

Create one new graphic, because no CDN graphic exists for this exact job: a single transparent-background cutout of an actual coyote, anatomically correct, unannounced, no clothing or human accessories, treated as a refined contemporary brush-and-flat-shape illustration. It must bridge the split seam at the lower-middle of the screen, partially covering the left headline and extending into the cabin photo. It should feel like a strange found presence, not a mascot or a logo. Make no second illustration. Use the spatial strategy of `https://media.withdraper.com/internal-references/14204d78-e202-49f2-9053-8e99945970c2/original.jpg` and the card-over-split idea of `https://media.withdraper.com/internal-references/14b1131d-4bac-4ef1-bcd9-e6d55b19703c/original.png` as layout references only. Use the actual Caven cabin image as the photographic content. The final frame should feel assembled and human because one element crosses two worlds, not because it is crowded or scrapbook-like.
