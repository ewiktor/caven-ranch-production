# I — paged river portal

Build a 1440 by 960 desktop homepage hero for Caven Ranch. This is a real first screen for a planned hospitality site, not a generic hotel landing page. The design must communicate that Caven Ranch is 1,000 acres of family-held Hill Country land on the Pedernales River and is becoming a small, design-led retreat. It must feel discovered, spatial, and deliberate, while carrying real information immediately.

Use the Slate color direction only: warm white `#F7F3F0` as the ground, near-black `#111111` as all type, and slate blue `#99A2AD` as the single color-field accent. Use Piazzolla 500 for display type at 64px with tight but natural tracking, and Familjen Grotesk 500 for navigation, labels, and small copy at 13px and 18px. Use mixed case only. Never use uppercase, coloured type, green, paper texture, drop shadows, technical brackets, decorative corner numbers, fake data labels, captions, or text directly over a photograph.

Structure the screen as a true portal with a useful editorial left column, not a full-bleed photograph with a line of copy on it. A narrow 72px top bar sits on warm white with `Caven Ranch` left and `the land`, `lodgings`, `stories`, and `marsha's list` right. Beneath it, make a 38 percent warm-white information column and a 62 percent visual sequence. The information column has generous left and bottom margins, a tiny sentence-case label `on the Pedernales River, outside Johnson City`, the exact display copy `This family has held this land for more than 150 years.`, and the exact supporting copy `A thousand acres along the Pedernales River are becoming a small, design-led retreat outside Johnson City.` Keep the text block compact and positioned low enough that the top half of the column remains active empty space.

The visual sequence is three adjacent, clearly designed parts. The main 52 percent image is the aerial river meander, cropped so the river pulls diagonally into the distance: `https://media.withdraper.com/1bc4729a-07cd-4572-98aa-8402c8bb5ea8/254e1a9220445fbdeac066ef72ee1b03a137342b4f24373beb0d9c76f646f796`. A 140px slate-blue paging rail sits to its right, with three sentence-case entries `01 the river`, `02 the cabins`, and `03 the land`; `01 the river` is active through weight and a simple filled square, never through a decorative rule. At the far right, show a deliberately cropped 180px preview of the next cabin image, using `https://media.withdraper.com/1bc4729a-07cd-4572-98aa-8402c8bb5ea8/3afdd839f49e8c95ce003a213a5e9c53734b40612cbcb85b8a2257defa55e002`, with `next: the cabins` on warm white below it. The next-image preview must feel like a genuine navigation device, not a card.

Reference the structural rhythm of the wide-image portal at `https://media.withdraper.com/1bc4729a-07cd-4572-98aa-8402c8bb5ea8/a542e3e617090354eccfd9827ed387fb913c304bfcad232bc503557c09ceb831` and the compact editorial header at `https://media.withdraper.com/internal-references/f6929940-4e6d-4cb8-8050-abec438fa1ca/original.jpg`, but do not place either reference image in the composition. The actual Caven river and cabin images above are the only photography. The final result should have the portal energy that Round 1 was reaching for, but with the proposition, navigation, and image sequencing built into the layout.
