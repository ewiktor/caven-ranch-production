# Experiences index as motion bands

V1 · 2026-07-17 · Expression [22 coyote motion sequence](../../../02%20expressions/22%20coyote%20motion%20sequence/doc.md), the anchored-list mode. Built to the user's comments on the tattoo-flash reference (hand-drawn-sequential-illustration-layout): rows where each on-property subject is drawn as multiple sequential frames of its own motion, with a handwritten label to the right. Honours the kills verbatim: NO prices, NO red dots / coloured bullet markers on the left, NOT vintage-litho / aged-paper (these silhouettes are clean and contemporary). Three motion bands are real hosted assets; the star row and the river line are simple enough to draw. Labels in Yellowtail (the loved script) kept as ink. All type is ink, no accent colour, no paper texture.

```
Design a desktop web section, 1440 x 1120. A single static layout.

THE ONE IDEA
The things you do here, each one drawn as its own motion. Every row is a subject caught in sequential frames, phase by phase across the page, like a motion study, with its name written by hand to the right. It reads like an index you would not see anywhere else: the animals and the land doing the describing, the words just naming them.

GROUND
Off white (#ECE7E3) across the full frame. No texture.

THE HEADER — top left, one lane at x=80
Small label at x=80, baseline y=90, "What you do here" in Yellowtail at 30px, deep black (#000A0A).
Under it, x=80, baseline y=150, a thin 60px rule, 1px, deep black (#000A0A) at 40 percent. This is the only rule on the page.

THE ROWS — five rows stacked down the page, each the same shape
Each row is a horizontal band of sequential illustration frames on the left running most of the width, and a handwritten name on the right. The bands sit on one shared left edge at x=80 and each is vertically centred on its row. The names sit on one shared right lane, right-aligned, ending at x=1360, in Yellowtail at 26px, deep black (#000A0A), mixed case. Each name is two short lines. There are no numbers, no bullets, no dots, and no prices anywhere.

Row 1 — top at y=210, band 720px wide:
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewiktor/caven-ranch-production/main/05%20graphics/assets/motion-bands/coyote-gait.png
  (five frames of a coyote trotting, black silhouette). Name, right-aligned ending x=1360: "the coyotes / you hear at night"

Row 2 — top at y=390, band 700px wide:
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewiktor/caven-ranch-production/main/05%20graphics/assets/motion-bands/bass-row.png
  (a row of four Guadalupe bass, black ink). Name: "the bass / in the Pedernales"

Row 3 — top at y=560, band 720px wide:
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewiktor/caven-ranch-production/main/05%20graphics/assets/motion-bands/monarch-wingbeat.png
  (six frames of a monarch's wingbeat, black ink). Name: "the monarchs / passing through"

Row 4 — top at y=740, a drawn star band:
  Draw a single horizontal row of small solid black five-pointed stars, evenly spaced, from x=80 running 720px wide, all the same size (about 22px), deep black (#000A0A). Name: "the sky / with nothing in the way"

Row 5 — top at y=910, a drawn river line:
  Draw a single long horizontal line in deep black (#000A0A), about 2px, running from x=80 for 720px, that gently rises and falls two or three times like a slow river or a low ridgeline. Name: "the river / where the limestone flattens out"

THE FOOT — bottom left, x=80, baseline y=1060
"Caven Ranch · what you do here" in Yellowtail at 20px, deep black (#000A0A) at 70 percent.

TYPE
Yellowtail throughout, at three sizes: the header, the row names, and the smaller foot. Mixed case. No uppercase, no other font.

COLOUR
Off white ground (#ECE7E3). Every illustration band and every piece of type is deep black ink (#000A0A). There is no accent colour anywhere and no paper texture. The whole page is ink on cream.

SPACING
Even vertical rhythm between the five rows, about 170px top to top, so the bands read as a steady list. The bands share a left edge at x=80 and the names share a right edge at x=1360, so two clean lanes hold the whole page. Use 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96 / 140.
```
