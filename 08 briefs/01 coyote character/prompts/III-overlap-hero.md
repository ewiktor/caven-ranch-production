# E01 coyote character — III: Painterly coyote breaking the split (home hero)

Build one desktop web hero, 1440 x 900.

**Content.** A home-page hero about the ranch itself: a serif headline on a cream field, a photographic block of the land, and a painterly illustrated coyote standing across the boundary between the two, half on the cream, half on the photo block, stitching the frame together. The animal is never mentioned in the copy, never labeled, never explained. It is simply there, crossing the seam, and the layout treats that as normal.

**Palette (count every color on screen, max 5).**
- Ground: warm white `#F7F3F0`, the left field (about 55% of the width).
- Ink (all type): near-black `#111111`.
- The photo block carries the only color moment (landscape greens and limestone).

**Type.** Piazzolla for everything except the single script word (weights 400, 500, 600 — display 600 here, and use the italic for the sub-line). Yellowtail for the word "Caven" only. No uppercase anywhere. Never an em dash. Max 4 sizes, from: 72 / 24 / 16 / 13.
- Wordmark top-left, 40px margin: "Caven" in Yellowtail 400, 32px... use 24px if 32 is unavailable on the scale — set it 24px, ink, followed on the same baseline by "Ranch" in Piazzolla 500, 24px, ink, 8px gap.
- Nav top-right, Piazzolla 500, 16px, ink, 40px gaps: "The land" · "The cabins" · "Stories" · "Marsha's list"
- Small label above the headline, Piazzolla 500, 13px, tracking +0.01em, ink: "Outside Johnson City, on the Pedernales"
- Headline, Piazzolla 600, 72px, line-height 1.05, tracking -0.02em, ink, max width 640px: "A thousand acres, held by one family for 150 years."
- Sub-line below, Piazzolla 400 italic, 24px, line-height 1.4, ink at 80%, max width 520px: "Now becoming a small, design-led place to stay, on the land's own terms."

**Image block.** Right side of the frame: a full-height photographic block, top and bottom bleed, starting at 55% width and running to the right edge, this asset with a 1px ink outline at 10% opacity on its left edge only:
`https://media.withdraper.com/1bc4729a-07cd-4572-98aa-8402c8bb5ea8/27d640dd308826036ee751bac7d30b066adecbfac8242258f372ca3918aabe00`
(rolling Hill Country scrubland and oak savanna, hazy hills.)

**The overlap (this is the point of this variation).** The coyote illustration stands ON the seam between cream field and photo block: roughly 40% of the animal over the cream, 60% over the photograph, bottom third of the frame, about 380px wide, facing into the photo (toward the land). It must genuinely cross the boundary and read in front of both fields. Generate it with Nano Banana Pro: "painterly illustrated coyote walking, side profile, dignified and calm, muted warm earth tones of tan and grey-brown, contemporary editorial illustration with visible soft brushwork, anatomically accurate, on a plain transparent background, no clothing, no accessories" — place the cutout so only the animal crosses the seam.

**Layout.** Headline block sits left, vertically centered on the cream, left margin 128px. Wordmark and nav on one top line. The coyote is the only element that crosses the seam. Air above and below the headline block must be unequal (more above) so the frame does not read centered-static. Every gap from: 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96 / 128 / 160.

**Do not:** no mention of the coyote in any copy, no uppercase, no em dashes, no watery blue illustration color, no clothing or accessories on the animal, no old/aged paper, no drop shadows, no captions on the image, no plate numbering, no Archivo, no Libre Caslon, no EB Garamond, no Geist, no Instrument Serif, type never colored and never green. The retreat is in development: no booking buttons, no "book now."
