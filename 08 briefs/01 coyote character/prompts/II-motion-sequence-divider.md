# E01 coyote character — II: Motion-sequence divider (web section)

Build one desktop web section, 1440 x 1024.

**Content.** A quiet mid-page section from the property/experience page about the land at night, where a horizontal row of sequential coyote silhouettes (chronophotography, distinct gait phases of one animal running) breaks the text in two. The animal is never explained or announced; it appears as a graphic rhythm between two pieces of writing, the way a divider would, and that is all.

**Palette (count every color on screen, max 5).**
- Ground: warm off-white `#ECE7E3`, whole frame.
- Ink (all type and five of the six silhouettes): near-black `#000A0A`.
- One accent moment only: exactly ONE silhouette in the row (the fourth of six) is rust `#813517`. Nothing else on the frame is colored.

**Type.** Piazzolla only (400, 500, and italic — show its range). No uppercase anywhere, sentence case only. Never an em dash. Max 4 sizes, from: 56 / 24 / 18 / 13.
- Small label, top-left of the section, Piazzolla 500, 13px, tracking +0.01em, ink: "The land, after dark"
- Section head below it, Piazzolla 500, 56px, line-height 1.1, tracking -0.02em, ink, max width 620px: "Nights out here still go fully dark."
- First body block, Piazzolla 400, 18px, line-height 1.6, ink, max width 560px: "There is no town glow to compete with, so the sky does all of it. The porch fire burns down, the crickets take over from the birds, and the stars come in thicker than most guests have ever seen them."
- Second body block (after the divider), same spec: "A few of them can sound like many. The Texas plains coyote moves at night, in a group, and out here you tend to hear it long before you see it."

**The divider (this is the point of this variation).** Between the two body blocks: a single horizontal row of six coyote silhouettes, each a different phase of one running gait (weight gathering, extension, full stretch, landing, recovery, trot), left to right, evenly spaced, each about 96px wide, baseline-aligned as if running on one invisible ground line. High-contrast flat solid silhouettes, anatomically true coyote (lean body, low straight tail, big ears), crisp contemporary vector-flat rendering. Never vintage lithograph, never aged print, never a grid contact sheet — one clean row. Generate the silhouettes with Nano Banana Pro: "six sequential motion-study silhouettes of a single coyote running, chronophotography gait phases, flat solid black shapes on transparent background, anatomically accurate coyote with low straight tail, side profile, clean contemporary vector-flat style, evenly spaced horizontal row" — then recolor the fourth frame rust `#813517`.

**Layout.** Everything hangs from a left text column starting at a 128px left margin. Label, 24px gap, headline, 40px gap, body one, 96px gap, the divider row (it may run wider than the text column, extending right toward the frame edge — let it break the column), 96px gap, body two. Generous open field on the right side of the text column; the divider is the only element allowed to cross into it. Every gap from: 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96 / 128 / 160.

**Do not:** no red dots or bullet chrome, no prices, no uppercase, no em dashes, no old/aged paper, no vintage-lithograph texture on the silhouettes, no explaining the animal or the divider, no captions, no plate numbering, no Archivo, no Libre Caslon, no EB Garamond, no Geist, no Instrument Serif, type never colored and never green (the one rust silhouette is the only colored element).
