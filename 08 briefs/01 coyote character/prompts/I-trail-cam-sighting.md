# E01 coyote character — I: Trail-cam sighting, faint (social 4:5)

Build one social post frame, 1080 x 1350 (4:5 portrait).

**Content.** An unexplained trail-camera capture of a coyote on the ranch, posted into the normal feed with zero explanation. It is never labeled a mascot, never named, never announced. The deadpan is the whole idea: a night photo, a timestamp, one quiet line. Nothing tells you why this is here.

**Palette (count every color on screen, max 5).**
- Ground: warm off-white `#ECE7E3`, whole frame.
- Ink (all type): near-black `#000A0A`.
- The photo is black-and-white. No colored background behind the animal anywhere.

**Type.** Piazzolla only (weights 400 and 500, optical size auto). No uppercase anywhere, sentence case only. Curly quotes if any. Never an em dash.
- Brand line top-left, 24px margin: "Caven Ranch" in Piazzolla 500, 16px, tracking 0, ink.
- Top-right, same baseline: "Texas Hill Country" in Piazzolla 400, 13px, ink at 55% opacity.
- Caption, bottom-left, 24px margin: Piazzolla 400 italic, 18px, line-height 1.5, ink: "Two fourteen in the morning, out by the river crossing."

**Image.** Generate the photo with Nano Banana Pro, then place it centered, contained (not full-bleed), width 920px: "black and white trail camera photograph at night, harsh direct flash, a single coyote mid-stride on rocky limestone ground with juniper brush behind, caught glancing toward the camera, eyes glowing from flash, burned-in white timestamp text 2023-10-27 02:14:33 AM in the bottom right corner, authentic infrared wildlife camera artifact quality, photographic grain" — 4:5 aspect.

**The faint treatment (this is the point of this variation).** The photo must read faded and quiet, not punchy: reduce contrast noticeably, lift the blacks toward `#2A2A28`, pull overall image opacity to about 88% against the warm ground, and let the warm off-white breathe through. It should feel like something half-remembered, not a poster. Add a very subtle paper-grain texture over the whole frame at 3 to 4% opacity (contemporary fine grain, never old paper, never foxing).

**Layout.** Brand line and location top. Big calm air between the header and the image (at least 96px). Image slightly above vertical center. Timestamp stays burned into the photo itself. Caption alone at the bottom. Whitespace is deliberate: top and bottom margins generous and unequal is fine, but every gap comes from this scale: 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96 / 128 / 160.

**Image framing.** Contained with a 1px ink outline at 12% opacity. No drop shadow. No caption ON the image. No rounded corners.

**Do not:** no color behind or around the animal, no uppercase, no em dashes, no old/aged/foxed paper, no plate or index numbering, no "meet our" or any explanation of the animal, no logo lockup beyond the words Caven Ranch, no Archivo, no Libre Caslon, no EB Garamond, no Geist, no Instrument Serif, type never colored and never green.
