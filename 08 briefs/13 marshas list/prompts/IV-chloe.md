Design a desktop web page: Marsha's List, for Caven Ranch. Frame 1440 x 1180px. A single static layout.

THE IDEA
An almost empty warm-cream page. A thin nav strip across the very top, then a vast open field. One centred line of serif type sits in the middle of that emptiness, with a small illustration directly beneath it. Photographs cling to the far corners and edges of the frame, several running off it, none of them anywhere near the centre. The emptiness is roughly two thirds of the page and it is the entire point: this is a quiet index of living things, and it does not crowd you.

GROUND
Warm cream #EBE8E0 across the full frame, with a very fine paper grain at 4 percent opacity. Felt, not seen.

THE NAV STRIP — full width, 96px tall, sitting flush at the very top
- Its own slightly lighter ground, #F2F0EA, meeting the page cream on a clean horizontal edge with no rule or border.
- Centred: "Caven Ranch" in Playfair Display, 26px, letter-spacing 0.08em, near-black #1C1815.
- Left group, starting x=96, in Playfair Display 15px, near-black, 40px between items: "Lodgings" · "Experiences" · "Stories"
- Right group, ending at x=1344, same style, 40px between items: "Marsha's List" · "Social"
- All of it sentence case, and quiet. The nav should read as a caption, not a header.

THE CENTRE — a single centred block at x=720, first baseline at y=560
Three lines in Playfair Display, 34px, line-height 1.5, near-black #1C1815, centred, with the named things set in true italic:
  line 1: A record of the *little bluestem*,
  line 2: *the Pedernales*, & the animals
  line 3: that were here first
(The words in asterisks are italic; the rest is roman. Use a real italic cut, not a slant. Keep the ampersand in italic, it is the nicest character on the page.)

THE MARK — directly beneath the centre block, centred at x=720, top at y=740
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewiktor/caven-ranch-production/main/05%20graphics/assets/paper-run-02/little-bluestem.png
(little bluestem grass, painted, transparent background). Render it small, 120px tall, sitting alone in the open space with nothing near it. It is a punctuation mark, not an image.

- One at the far right edge at x=1300 y=440, 260 x 300px, running off the right side of the frame.
- One right side at x=1180 y=930, 210 x 300px.
- One bottom-right at x=980 y=1050, 290 x 240px, running off the bottom edge.
- One upper-right at x=1050 y=170, 150 x 190px, small and easy to miss.
- One top edge at x=620 y=-30, 170 x 150px, running off the top of the field just below the nav.
Every one is a plain rectangle, edge to edge, no hairline and no shadow. No two share an alignment. Rotate two of them by 1 degree. Sizes vary widely on purpose: the 150px one and the 300px one should feel like different kinds of object.
