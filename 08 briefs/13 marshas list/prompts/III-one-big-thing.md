# III — one big thing · Bodoni Moda

All the weight in one place, violent scale jump, name running vertically over the image. Display face: Bodoni Moda (sharp high-contrast didone). Positive spec only.

```
Design a desktop web page: Marsha's List, for Caven Ranch. Frame 1440 x 1000px. A single static layout.

THE IDEA
One photograph is enormous and sits right of centre. Everything else on the page is tiny and scattered around it. The scale jump is violent and deliberate: one thing at 620px, the rest at 100 to 160px. The name is set vertically, running up the right side of the big image and lying across it. It reads like one specimen pulled out and studied while the rest of the collection waits around it.

GROUND
Warm white #F7F3F0 across the full frame, with a whisper of paper grain at 3 percent opacity.

THE BIG ONE — 620 x 780px at x=520 y=120, running off the bottom edge of the frame
https://media.withdraper.com/1bc4729a-07cd-4572-98aa-8402c8bb5ea8/3347d2354c58a082fd1f6dc8581d9db4a0c78f41ba4bf4e64449be771cea3a0f
(eroded limestone shelves descending into pale teal and cerulean water). Full bleed within its own rectangle, edge to edge, no hairline.

THE NAME — vertical, rotated 90 degrees counter-clockwise, running up the right edge of the big photograph at about x=1180
- "Marsha's List" in Bodoni Moda, 60px, weight 500.
- It lies directly across the photograph, half on the image and half on the cream. Where it crosses the image it is warm white #F7F3F0; where it crosses the ground it is near-black #12100E. One continuous line, one colour change at the image edge.
- This is the only text on the page.

THE SMALL ONES — six images, 100px to 160px wide, in the left third and along the top
Cluster three loosely in the upper-left, two down the left edge, and tuck one so it overlaps the bottom-right corner of the big photograph. Freely placed, no shared alignment. Rotate one by 2 degrees. Give each a 1px #969581 hairline at 35 percent opacity.
```
