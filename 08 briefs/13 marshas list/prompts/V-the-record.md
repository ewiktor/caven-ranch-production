V1 · 2026-07-17 · Built on the full feedback of record: Round 2 Group 13 ("canvas holds THINGS, not images"; scribble identifier asked for twice; artifact tags kept; numbering/labels killed) + the 24 marshas list identity locks (gyotaku fabric stamp, scattered canvas with central anchor). Two new assets made and hosted for this prompt: the scribble identifier and the rust gyotaku bass.

```
Design a desktop web page: Marsha's List, for Caven Ranch. Frame 1440 x 1190px. A single static layout.

THE IDEA
An almost empty warm off-white page. A thin quiet nav strip at the top, then a vast open field. At the centre sits the identity of Marsha's List — a hand-scribbled ink signature with three short serif lines beneath it. Scattered far out at the edges are four THINGS from the ranch's living record: a fish printed on fabric, a painted bird, a stalk of prairie grass, a relic. Each thing is a different kind of object and none of them is framed, matted, or contained — they sit directly on the page the way objects sit on a table. The emptiness between them is most of the design: this is a record you wander, not a grid you scan.

GROUND
Warm off-white #ECE7E3 across the full frame, with a very fine even grain at 4 percent opacity. Felt, not seen.

THE NAV STRIP — full width, 92px tall, flush at the top
- Slightly lighter ground #F2F0EA meeting the page on a clean horizontal edge, no rule.
- Centred: "Caven Ranch" in Piazzolla, 24px, near-black #000A0A, mixed case.
- Left group starting x=96, Piazzolla 15px, 44px apart: "Lodgings" · "Experiences" · "Stories"
- Right group ending x=1344, same style: "Marsha's List" · "Social" · "Book"
- All sentence case. The nav reads as a caption, not a header.

THE CENTRE — the identity block, centred on x=720
- The identifier: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewiktor/caven-ranch-production/main/05%20graphics/assets/marshas-list/marshas-list-scribble-v1.png
  (hand-scribbled "Marsha's List" ink signature, transparent background). Width 380px, centred, its vertical middle at y=470. This is the logo of the record — give it room; nothing comes within 120px of it.
- Beneath the signature, 44px gap, three centred lines in Piazzolla, 21px, line-height 1.6, near-black #000A0A, mixed case, the named things in true italic:
    A record of the living things on this land —
    the *little bluestem*, the *golden-cheeked warbler*,
    the *bass* in the Pedernales, the *coyotes* you hear at night.

THE THINGS — four objects in the outer band, every one at least 400px from the centre of the signature. No frames, no mats, no shadows, no numbering.
1. Upper-left at x=170 y=150, 235 x 295px — the fabric print, a swatch of cloth sitting flat on the page:
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewiktor/caven-ranch-production/main/05%20graphics/assets/marshas-list/gyotaku-guadalupe-bass-rust-v1.png
   (Guadalupe bass pressed in rust ink into woven cream fabric, the fish wrapping top and bottom edge, its name stamped small on the cloth). It carries its own label — add nothing to it.
2. Left edge at x=55 y=560, 175 x 220px — the painted bird:
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewiktor/caven-ranch-production/main/05%20graphics/assets/paper-run-01/13-warbler-ink-v1.png
   (golden-cheeked warbler, ink and watercolor on cream). Beneath it, centred: a 24px-wide hairline in walnut #432F2E, then 8px below, "golden-cheeked warbler" in a typewriter mono, 11px, walnut #432F2E, lowercase.
3. Right edge at x=1160 y=420, 200 x 265px — the relic:
   https://media.withdraper.com/1bc4729a-07cd-4572-98aa-8402c8bb5ea8/60899228b6f2ee157aeab16360070575f23bd9482a972569c434c876dbf5596b
   (patinated brass artillery shell, hand-etched cavalry soldier — a photograph, because a relic is a photographed thing where an animal is a drawn one). Beneath it, centred: the same 24px walnut hairline, then "relic of the Great War" in the typewriter mono, 11px, walnut, lowercase.
4. Lower-right at x=1010 y=880, about 300px tall, running off the bottom edge — the grass itself, a loose cutout with no rectangle around it:
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewiktor/caven-ranch-production/main/05%20graphics/assets/paper-run-02/little-bluestem.png
   (little bluestem, painted, transparent background). No label — it is the one thing allowed to just grow out of the edge of the page.
The four things are four different kinds of object — cloth, painting, photograph, cutout — and their sizes contrast hard. No two share an alignment.

TYPE
Piazzolla for everything except the two small labels, which are a typewriter mono. Mixed case and sentence case everywhere. Serif type in near-black #000A0A, labels in walnut #432F2E.

COLOUR
Ground #ECE7E3. Nav strip #F2F0EA. Ink near-black #000A0A. Rust #813517 arrives only inside the fabric print. Walnut #432F2E for hairlines and labels. Nothing else.
```
