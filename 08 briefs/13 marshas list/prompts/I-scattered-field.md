# I — scattered field · Prata

Weight everywhere, type in the middle holding it. Display face: Prata (high-contrast editorial serif). Positive spec only.

```
Design a desktop web page: Marsha's List, for Caven Ranch. Frame 1440 x 1000px. A single static layout.

THE IDEA
A warm cream field with photographs of the ranch's land scattered across it at wildly different scales, and the name sitting in the middle holding the whole thing down. It looks like a collection someone actually keeps. Nothing aligns to anything. The air between things is most of the design.

GROUND
Warm cream #ECE7E3 across the full frame, with a very soft paper presence: a fine, even grain at 4 percent opacity. Barely there. Someone should feel it rather than see it.

THE ANCHOR — centred, roughly x=720 y=470
- "Marsha's List" in Prata, 68px, near-black #1A1614, tracking -0.01em.
- Below it, 24px gap: "the plants, the water, and the animals that were here first" in Prata 19px, centred, max-width 380px, line-height 1.5.
- This is the only text on the page.

THE SCATTER — nine images, freely placed, scales from 90px to 420px wide
Push the scale contrast hard: a 420px image sitting near a 90px one. Rotate two or three of them by 1 to 2 degrees. Let three run off the frame edge. Let two overlap at a corner. The nearest images come within about 60px of the centre type so it feels held. Each image is a plain rectangle with a 1px #919D9D hairline at 30 percent opacity.

TYPE
Prata throughout, in sentence case. Set in near-black #1A1614.

COLOUR
Ground warm cream #ECE7E3. Type near-black #1A1614. Hairlines #919D9D. The photographs carry all the colour in the frame.

SPACING
Use 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96 / 128 / 160. Keep gaps inside a group tight and gaps between groups wide.
```
