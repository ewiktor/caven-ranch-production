```
Design a desktop web section, 1440 x 820. A single static layout.

THE ONE IDEA
The drive is the arrival, told like a ranger's hand-drawn note. A loose ink route wanders from Austin across the whole frame to the cabin, crossing the seam between the two cream halves so the page reads as one journey.

THE SPLIT — two tints of cream, no line between them
Left half, x=0 to x=720: warm sand (#E7DECF).
Right half, x=720 to x=1440: off white (#ECE7E3).
They meet on a clean vertical butt edge at x=720. The value change is the only divider.

THE ROUTE — one continuous HAND-DRAWN line, thin deep-black (#000A0A), crossing the whole frame including the seam
Draw it like ink on paper: a slightly wobbly, uneven route, not a clean vector, about 2px with natural weight variation. It starts far left around x=120 y=240 and rambles down and right through loose bends to the cabin at about x=1030 y=560, continuing unbroken where it crosses x=720. A few short hatch ticks along it, like a trail marked by hand.

Waypoints, each a small hand-inked triangle or dot with a label beside it in Special Elite at 13px, mixed case, deep black (#000A0A), and a loose hand-drawn leader:
- x=120 y=240 — "Austin"
- x=380 y=350 — "290, going west"
- x=560 y=460 — "Johnson City"
- x=740 y=520 — "the two-lane" (sits on the seam, its leader crosses onto the off-white side)
- x=900 y=545 — "the last gate"
The destination at x=1030 y=560 is a hand-drawn pin, filled rust (#813517), about 22px, with "your cabin" beside it in Special Elite at 14px deep black. The pin is the only colour on the drawn map.
One playful aside, hand-lettered small near a bend at about x=470 y=410, in Special Elite at 12px, deep black: "turkeys, usually." No leader, just dropped by the road like a note.

THE COMPASS — top left, at about x=140 y=110
A minimal hand-inked "N" in Special Elite at 14px with a short wobbly up-arrow above it.

THE CONTENT — upper right, on the off-white side, one hard left lane at x=800
Headline, first baseline y=210, Petrona at 50px, weight 500, line-height 54px, deep black (#000A0A), mixed case, two lines with the leading tight, one word in true italic:
  The road does the
  *unwinding* for you.
Directly under it, at x=800 y=250, a small onward ">" in Petrona at 30px, rust (#813517).

THE PHOTO INSET — on the off-white side, x=800, top edge y=300
A plain square, 320 x 320px, object-fit cover, sitting directly on the off white. No border, no shadow, no rounding.
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewiktor/caven-ranch-production/main/05%20graphics/assets/paper-run-01/12-drive-tunnel-v1.png
(a pale gravel lane running into a dark tunnel of Ashe juniper, the view ahead hidden by the trees). Its left edge sits on the x=800 lane.

THE BODY — x=800, width 360px, first baseline y=680
Four to five lines in DM Sans at 13px, line-height 20px, deep black (#000A0A), mixed case:
"The four-lane gives out to a two-lane, and the two-lane gives out to gravel under a low run of juniper. You lose the view in the trees and you do not get it back until you are standing on the cabin ramp. By then you have already slowed all the way down."

THE FOOT — x=800, baseline y=780
"Caven Ranch · the drive in" in DM Sans at 12px, mixed case, walnut (#432F2E).

TYPE
Petrona for the headline, with a true italic on the one word. Special Elite for the small map labels, the aside, and the compass only, never large. DM Sans for body and foot. Mixed case everywhere.

COLOUR
Warm sand (#E7DECF) left, off white (#ECE7E3) right. All ink and type deep black (#000A0A). Rust (#813517) appears exactly twice: the destination pin and the onward ">". The photograph carries the rest.

SPACING
The left map field is mostly empty, and the emptiness is the distance. Content sits in one lane at x=800. Use 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96 / 140.
```
