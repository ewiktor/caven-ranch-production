# III — the map runs into the place (flipped split · contemporary grotesk · photo bleed)

V1 · 2026-07-17 · Same concept as I and II, but the split is flipped and the far side is a full-bleed destination photo: the drawn route bleeds off the cream and runs straight into the real place, so the map ends and the photograph begins at the same edge. Axis moved: composition (map runs into a bled photo rather than a small inset) and a third type voice (Space Grotesk, the cool contemporary/Austin register). Palette accent Direction 3 (Sky Blue). Tests whether the arrival lands harder as a full image than as an inset.

```
Design a desktop web section, 1440 x 820. A single static layout.

THE ONE IDEA
The map runs into the real place. The drawn route crosses the cream field and, right where the country actually opens, the drawing gives way to a full-bleed photograph of it. The seam between map and photo is the moment the view arrives.

THE SPLIT — cream on the left, a full-bleed photograph on the right
Left, x=0 to x=880: warm white (#F7F3F0), holding the content and the map.
Right, x=880 to x=1440, full height: a single photograph running full bleed, touching the top, right, and bottom edges, no margin, no border, no rounding, object-fit cover.

(a person walking down the oxide-red steel ramp along the cedar cabin wall, the Hill Country opening in front of them). Crop so the opening country and the ramp read.

THE ROUTE — one continuous drawn line, thin coal (#111111), running left to right and INTO the photo edge
A clean route, about 1.5px, starting far left around x=90 y=300 and travelling right through a few bends to about x=870 y=470, where it meets the photograph's left edge and stops as if it walked off the map into the picture. Drawn, not photographic.

Waypoints, small coal triangles with labels in Space Grotesk at 12px, mixed case, coal (#111111), each with a 14px hairline leader (0.75px):
- x=90 y=300 — "Austin"
- x=300 y=380 — "290 west"
- x=500 y=440 — "Johnson City"
- x=700 y=470 — "the two-lane"
At the point where the route meets the photo, x=860 y=470, a map pin filled sky blue-deep (#8FB0C0), about 22px, no label. The pin sits half on the cream and half over the photo edge, marking the hand-off. It is the only colour on the drawn map.

THE COMPASS — top left, x=110 y=110
A minimal "N" in Space Grotesk at 13px coal with a short up-arrow above it.

THE CONTENT — upper left, one hard left lane at x=90
Wordmark at x=90 y=56, "Caven Ranch" in Space Grotesk at 20px, weight 600, mixed case, coal (#111111).
Headline, first baseline y=180, Space Grotesk at 48px, weight 600, line-height 52px, coal (#111111), mixed case, two lines tight:
  You do not see it

A small onward ">" directly under, x=90 y=222, Space Grotesk at 28px, sky blue-deep (#8FB0C0).

THE BODY — x=90, width 380px, first baseline y=680
Four to five lines in DM Sans at 13px, line-height 20px, coal (#111111), mixed case:

THE FOOT — x=90, baseline y=780
"the drive in" in DM Sans at 12px, mixed case, sky blue-grey (#7C8E98).

TYPE
Space Grotesk for the wordmark, headline, waypoint labels, and compass. DM Sans for body and foot. Mixed case everywhere.

COLOUR
Warm white (#F7F3F0) on the left. All drawn line-work and type coal (#111111). Sky blue-deep (#8FB0C0) appears exactly twice: the hand-off pin and the onward ">". The full-bleed photograph carries every other colour.

SPACING
The map field is open, and the route travels a long quiet way before it reaches the photo. Content in one lane at x=90. Use 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96 / 140.
```
