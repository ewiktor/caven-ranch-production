# I — the specimen grid

V1 · 2026-07-17 · Expression exploration for [25 cabin architecture](../../../02%20expressions/25%20cabin%20architecture/doc.md). Axis: image conduct. Many tight crops of matter, the building withheld entirely. Palette roll: Direction 4b (Ice). Written for Claude Design / Figma Make, so no links: every image is described.

```
Design a desktop web page section, 1440 x 1000. A single static layout.

THE IDEA
A materials page for a cabin you never see. The whole page is what the thing is made of: tight photographic crops of surface, named, placed across a bone field with a lot of air between them. One enormous serif word holds the left. The matter carries every bit of colour on the page, so the page itself stays nearly neutral and lets cedar be warm and limestone be white.

GROUND
Bone (#F6F6F2) across the full frame.

THE NAV STRIP — full width, 76px tall, flush at the top
Its own ground of ice blue (#DFE8F3), meeting the bone on a clean horizontal edge. "Caven Ranch" sits at the left, 40px in from the edge, in Gilda Display at 19px, mixed case, smoke black (#2F2E31). Four links sit across the right, ending 40px from the right edge, 40px apart, in Work Sans at 13px, mixed case, smoke black: "Lodgings", "The Land", "Stories", "Marsha's List". The strip is the only cool colour on the page and it makes the materials below it read warmer.

THE WORD — left, low
"Materials" in Gilda Display, about 92px, mixed case, smoke black (#2F2E31), starting 60px from the left edge with its baseline at about y=560. Slightly tightened tracking. It sits lower than you expect and the space above it is deliberate.

THE SPECIMENS — tight square crops of surface, placed in an uneven scatter across the right two thirds
Each crop carries its name directly above it in Work Sans at 11px, lowercase, letter-spacing about 0.14em, smoke black. Each crop has a 1px border of matt sage (#C7C4B0) at 40 percent.

An upper row of four, sitting between x=640 and x=1400, tops varying between y=200 and y=250 so the row is level-ish but not ruled:
- "cedar" — extreme close-up of vertical cedar plank siding, warm brown, the grain running top to bottom, 170 x 170px
- "limestone" — white Hill Country limestone, rough and irregular, dry and chalky, 160 x 175px
- "corten steel" — weathered oxide-red steel, deep rust surface, 175 x 165px
- "fieldstone" — dry-stack fieldstone masonry, grey and tan stones fitted without mortar, 165 x 170px

A lower pair, sitting under the word at around x=140 and x=290, y=700:
- "concrete" — a cast concrete pier, smooth grey with faint form marks, 140 x 150px
- "glass" — a large pane in a thin dark frame, reflecting juniper and sky, 140 x 150px

One lone crop, far right and lower than everything else, at about x=1230, y=760, 150 x 155px:
- "steel mesh" — fine wire mesh railing infill, taut, catching light

Its isolation is the point. Nothing sits near it.

THE LINE — bottom left, 60px from the left edge, baseline at about y=940
"The first cabin is in development. This is what it is being built from." in Work Sans at 13px, mixed case, smoke black (#2F2E31).

TYPE
Gilda Display for the word and the wordmark. Work Sans for every small label and link. Mixed case and lowercase throughout.

COLOUR
Bone ground (#F6F6F2). Nav strip ice blue (#DFE8F3). Type and wordmark smoke black (#2F2E31). Crop borders matt sage (#C7C4B0) at 40 percent. Every other colour in the frame comes from the materials themselves.

SPACING
Generous. The gap between the word and the nearest crop is the widest gap on the page. Use 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96 / 140.
```
