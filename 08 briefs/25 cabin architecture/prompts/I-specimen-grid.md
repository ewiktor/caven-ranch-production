# I — the specimen grid

V2 · 2026-07-17 · Every image slot now carries a live link (V1 shipped descriptions, which breaks the assets-before-prompts rule). The six material specimens were generated and hosted for this prompt; the wide shot is real media. Expression: [25 cabin architecture](../../../02%20expressions/25%20cabin%20architecture/doc.md). Axis: image conduct — tight crops of matter, the building withheld until the very bottom. Palette roll: Direction 4b (Ice).

```
Design a desktop web page section, 1440 x 1120. A single static layout.

THE IDEA
A materials page for a cabin you barely see. The page is what the thing is made of: tight photographic crops of surface, named, placed across a bone field with a lot of air between them. One enormous serif word holds the left. The matter carries every bit of colour, so the page itself stays nearly neutral and lets cedar be warm and limestone be white.

GROUND
Bone (#F6F6F2) across the full frame.

THE NAV STRIP — full width, 76px tall, flush at the top
Its own ground of ice blue (#DFE8F3), meeting the bone on a clean horizontal edge. "Caven Ranch" at the left, 40px in, in Gilda Display at 19px, mixed case, smoke black (#2F2E31). Four links across the right, ending 40px from the right edge, 40px apart, in Work Sans at 13px, mixed case, smoke black: "Lodgings", "The Land", "Stories", "Marsha's List". This strip is the only cool colour on the page and it makes the materials below read warmer.

THE WORD — left, low
"Materials" in Gilda Display, about 92px, mixed case, smoke black (#2F2E31), starting 60px from the left edge with its baseline at about y=560. Slightly tightened tracking. It sits lower than you expect and the space above it is deliberate.

THE SPECIMENS — square crops, placed in an uneven scatter across the right two thirds
Each carries its name directly above it in Work Sans at 11px, lowercase, letter-spacing about 0.14em, smoke black (#2F2E31). Each has a 1px border of matt sage (#C7C4B0) at 40 percent. Render each image with object-fit cover.

Its isolation is the point. Nothing sits near it.

THE ONE WIDE FRAME — bottom right, at about x=880 y=900, 500 x 190px, a letterbox crop
The only picture of the building on the page, and it arrives last and small, after all the matter:

(the cabin on its steel columns with a fieldstone base, limestone slabs and a fire pit in front). Render with object-fit cover, cropped to the letterbox, with the same 1px matt sage border. No label.

THE LINE — bottom left, 60px from the left edge, baseline at about y=1000
"The first cabin is in development. This is what it is being built from." in Work Sans at 13px, mixed case, smoke black (#2F2E31).

TYPE
Gilda Display for the word and the wordmark. Work Sans for every label, link, and line. Mixed case and lowercase throughout.

COLOUR
Bone ground (#F6F6F2). Nav strip ice blue (#DFE8F3). Type smoke black (#2F2E31). Borders matt sage (#C7C4B0) at 40 percent. Every other colour comes from the materials.

SPACING
Generous. The gap between the word and the nearest specimen is the widest gap on the page. Use 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96 / 140.
```
