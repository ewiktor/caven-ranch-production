# II — the section

V1 · 2026-07-17 · Expression exploration for [25 cabin architecture](../../../02%20expressions/25%20cabin%20architecture/doc.md). Axis: content-to-form. The cantilever explained as a drawing, materials keyed to it with leader lines (the leader line is an explicit keeper from the heritage Round-2 feedback). Photography withheld entirely. Palette roll: Direction 3 (Sky Blue). Written for Claude Design / Figma Make, so no links: the drawing is described.

```
Design a desktop web page section, 1440 x 1000. A single static layout.

THE IDEA
The cabin explained as a drawing rather than a photograph. One clean architectural section cuts through the hillside and the building, and thin leader lines run from it out to material names set in small type. It reads like something from the architect's set: measured, unglamorous, and confident enough not to need a hero shot. The page is proving how the thing works, not how it photographs.

GROUND
Warm white (#F7F3F0) across the full frame.

THE NAV STRIP — full width, 76px tall, flush at the top
Warm white ground, with a 1px khaki (#C5BA8C) hairline running the full width along its bottom edge. "Caven Ranch" at the left, 48px in, in Spectral at 19px, mixed case, coal black (#111111). Four links across the right, ending 48px from the right edge, 40px apart, in Schibsted Grotesk at 13px, mixed case, coal black: "Lodgings", "The Land", "Stories", "Marsha's List".

THE HEADLINE — centred, first baseline at about y=160
Two lines in Spectral, 40px, line-height 54px, coal black (#111111), centred, mixed case:
  "It leans out over the hill,
  then sets you back in the trees."

THE DRAWING — the centre of the page, occupying roughly x=300 to x=1140, y=280 to y=790
A single architectural section drawn in thin coal-black lines on the warm white, no fill and no shading except where noted. It shows, left to right: a hillside falling away; a limestone-and-gravel courtyard at the entry level on the high side with a low wall screening the road; a ramp running down the side of the building; the cabin itself set on slender steel pilotis with the ground beneath it left open; a pitched roof with deep eaves; the front living space cantilevered out past the last pilotis so it hangs over the falling ground, its outer wall entirely glass; a small nook jutting out from the main footprint; and the bedroom at the back, set into the hillside where the cabin meets the land, with tree canopy drawn loosely around it.

The line weight is even and fine, about 1.25px, like a drafted section. The one filled element: the sky beyond the glass wall is a soft field of sky blue (#CBDEE6), and it is the only fill on the page. It sits where the cantilever hangs, so the colour lands exactly where the idea does.

The trees around the bedroom end are drawn loosely, with a lighter hand than the building.

THE LEADER LINES — thin coal-black hairlines, about 0.75px, running from a small dot on the drawing out to a label
Each label is Schibsted Grotesk at 11px, lowercase, letter-spacing 0.1em, coal black (#111111). The lines run horizontally or at a shallow angle, never crossing each other. Labels sit outside the drawing, in the left and right margins and along the bottom:
- "cedar siding" — to the outer wall
- "pitched roof, deep eaves" — to the roof
- "glass, facing west" — to the cantilevered glass wall
- "the reading nook" — to the jutting nook
- "the bedroom, in the trees" — to the back of the plan
- "steel pilotis" — to the slender columns
- "limestone and gravel courtyard" — to the entry level
- "the ramp down" — to the ramp
- "open ground beneath" — to the space between the beams

THE BASE — a row of three short blocks, sitting across the bottom, tops at about y=850
Each is Schibsted Grotesk at 13px, line-height 21px, coal black (#111111), max-width 300px. They sit at x=300, x=630, and x=960, aligned to the drawing's left edge and spaced evenly:
- "The pitched roof is a traditional form. It keeps the cabin from reading as too modern, or as a spaceship on a hill."
- "The cantilever is doing the work. The front living space pushes you out over the landscape, and the bedroom sets you back into the hillside."
- "The first cabin is in development, and a small number will follow. The low count is the plan, not a phase."

TYPE
Spectral for the wordmark and the headline. Schibsted Grotesk for every label and the base blocks. Mixed case and lowercase throughout.

COLOUR
Warm white ground (#F7F3F0). All line work and type coal black (#111111). Sky blue (#CBDEE6) once, as the sky behind the glass. Khaki (#C5BA8C) once, as the nav hairline.

SPACING
The drawing gets the room. Use 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96 / 140.
```
