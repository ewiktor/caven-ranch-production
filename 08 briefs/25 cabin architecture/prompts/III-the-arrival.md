# III — the arrival

V1 · 2026-07-17 · Expression exploration for [25 cabin architecture](../../../02%20expressions/25%20cabin%20architecture/doc.md). Axis: eye path and the withheld thing. The arrival sequence as five moments, left to right; the wide shot of the cabin never appears. Draws on the richest narrative material the cabins have (`06 knowledge/property-details-2.md`). Palette roll: Direction 2 (Rust). Written for Claude Design / Figma Make, so no links: every image is described.

```
Design a desktop web page section, 1440 x 1000. A single static layout.

THE IDEA
Walking in, in five moments, left to right. Tall narrow frames like a strip of film, each one a step of the approach: the courtyard, the wall, the ramp, the view opening, the turn. You never see the whole cabin from outside, because the point is that the building reveals itself in order, and the page arrives the same way the person does. The frames step down and up slightly as they cross, so the eye walks rather than scans.

Off white (#ECE7E3) across the full frame.
```
