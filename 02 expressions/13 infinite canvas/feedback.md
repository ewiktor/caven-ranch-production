From the user's Creative Concepts doc (Notion):

> "A free-flowing canvas experience on the website. You click into things, images of animals, illustrations of plants, graphics, conservation stories, family artifacts, whatever belongs, and each one opens up and tells a little story about that thing."

From the reference library:

> "An expansive, nonlinear grid resembles a physical mood board or digital infinite canvas. Assets vary in scale and overlap organically, with central typography acting as an anchor. Generous negative space keeps the discovery experience from feeling cluttered." — infinite-scroll-ephemera-canvas

From the user's Expressions Catalogue v4 (months old):

> "This content concept creates a digital desk or archive view where users explore scattered artifacts like pressed flowers, botanical sketches, and family relics as if they were rifling through a physical drawer. It mimics the tactile feeling of history by presenting items with their natural imperfections… It emphasizes the found nature of the family history in a tactile and non-linear way that invites users to piece together the story of the ranch through organic discovery and curated clutter." — Women's List - Flora / Fauna
