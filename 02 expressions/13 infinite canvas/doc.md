# Infinite canvas — expressions doc

**Version:** V1 · 2026-07-16 · Formed at second board pass, from the user's Creative Concepts doc (Marsha's List) + the ephemera-canvas reference + the old catalogue's desk-archive concept
**Type:** brand expression (layout / web mechanic — the discovery surface)

## What it is

A free-flowing, nonlinear canvas the visitor wanders: scattered artifacts — animal images, plant illustrations, graphics, conservation stories, family relics — each clickable, each opening into a little story. A digital desk or archive drawer, "rifling through" rather than scrolling. It is the mechanic behind Marsha's List, and — if the direction goes bold — potentially how the entire site is navigated.

## What the user likes

"An expansive, nonlinear grid resembles a physical mood board or digital infinite canvas. Assets vary in scale and overlap organically, with central typography acting as an anchor. Generous negative space keeps the discovery experience from feeling cluttered." The tactile-archive read: items with "their natural imperfections, such as the delicate root structures of dried plants… rather than flat scans or polished vector icons." "The canvas should feel like something you want to spend time clicking around in."

## Control for (locked)

- **Zones (structure):** nonlinear scatter, organic overlap, varying scales; a typographic anchor holding the field
- **Space:** generous negative space — curated clutter, never actual clutter
- **Imagery:** artifacts presented as physical things (pressed flowers, sketches, relics) with real imperfections
- **Motion/interaction:** items open into their stories on click
