# Offset stack layout — expressions doc

**Version:** V1 · 2026-07-16 · Split out of the first pass's "editorial layout" at the user's direction to make the layout systems concrete
**Type:** brand expression (layout system)

## What it is

The layered depth system: images offset and stacked with different opacities, multiply blends, and staggered positions, anchored by large serif typography — a print-collage depth that can sit still or move ([09 site motion](../09%20site%20motion/doc.md) animates it). One of the brand's concrete layout moves, under the negative-space stance ([04](../04%20negative%20space%20layout/doc.md)).

## What the user likes

"Monochromatic images are offset and stacked with different opacities to create depth… transparency, multiply effects, a structured interface grid, and muted olive and ochre tones create an editorial motion system." Overlap as rhythm, not clutter — the stagger keeps reading order.

## Control for (locked)

- **Depth:** stacked layers with visible offset; opacity/multiply doing the depth work, not drop shadows
- **Type:** large serif anchor over or under the stack; small structured metadata
- **Color:** monochromatic or tightly-related image tones per stack (from [05 palette](../05%20palette/doc.md) registers)

## Leave open — filled + cycled (variation space)

- **Stack density (focus control):** two-layer whisper / multi-layer collage — cycle
- **Static vs. staggered motion:** rides [09 site motion](../09%20site%20motion/doc.md)
- **What stacks (filled):** property imagery, archival material, illustration — from `03 media/` and the expression systems
