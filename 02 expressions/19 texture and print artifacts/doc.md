# Texture and print artifacts — expressions doc

**Version:** V1 · 2026-07-16 · Formed at second board pass — the surface-material system pulled out of the graphic expressions so every surface can cite one place
**Type:** brand expression (texture)

## What it is

The material grammar of every brand surface: textured paper grounds, film grain, halftone and riso grain, stipple distress, print-proof residue (crop marks, registration details), patina and earned wear. It exists so "tactile" is a controlled system rather than a vibe — the brand's surfaces read as printed, handled, and made, against the flat-vector default of hospitality branding.

## What the user likes

Paper that participates: "textured paper, pooled pigment," "visible paper grain," "paper texture gives the palette a tactile quality." Print-shop residue as aesthetic: "halftone dithering, ghosted frames, crop marks, registration details." Wear that is earned, not applied: oxidation, foxing, creases on genuinely old things ([03 heritage ephemera](../03%20heritage%20ephemera/doc.md) holds that rule).

## Control for (locked)

- **Texture (grounds):** textured paper as the default ground register; grain subtle and tactile, never dirty
- **Texture (print):** halftone, riso grain, stipple, crop marks/registration as deliberate devices tied to the print metaphor
- **Honesty rule:** distress belongs to actually-old artifacts and print-process styles; new things do not get fake aging (per the heritage expression's earned-wear-only lock)

## Leave open — filled + cycled (variation space)

- **Intensity (focus control):** whisper (2–4% grain) → present (visible tooth) → loud (riso/print-proof capsule surfaces) — cycle per surface
- **Which metaphor leads:** paper / print-proof / textile (the shibori-embroidered direction in [02](../02%20illustration%20language/doc.md)) — cycle

## New references — 2026-07-17 (bake-in)

Both push texture toward **contemporary material** (fabric, mixed pattern), not aged paper — consistent with the settled no-scrapbook / no-old-paper constraint.

- [pattern-interplay-and-vertical-typography](../../04%20references/pattern-interplay-and-vertical-typography.md) — "Mixing patterns with imagery... use the layout and mix some type of texture or some type of pattern with imagery." New open direction: pattern/texture mixed against photography with vertical text at top. Kill: "I don't like it being in a scrapbook" — take the layout, drop the scrapbook framing.
- [stamped-fabric-gyotaku-illustration](../../04%20references/stamped-fabric-gyotaku-illustration.md) — "It doesn't look like paper. It looks more like fabric." Sharpened lock: the texture register is stamped-on-fabric, not paper — a woven-cloth ground rather than an aged-print surface (shared with [02 illustration language](../02%20illustration%20language/doc.md)).
