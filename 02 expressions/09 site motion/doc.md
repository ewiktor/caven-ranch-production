# Site motion — expressions doc

**Version:** V1 · 2026-07-16 · Formed at second board pass, from the user's Creative Concepts doc + the motion-layout references
**Type:** brand expression (web motion — site-wide behavior)

## What it is

The site-wide motion language: animated transitions between pages, images spreading apart to reveal content, sticky scroll, staggered reveals, scroll-driven sequences. Explicitly a different thing from the preloader ([08](../08%20preloader/doc.md)), which is one moment; this is how the whole site moves. It exists because the brand adds motion and interactivity on top of the Ett Hem simplicity baseline — that is one of the named ways Caven Ranch builds up from "start simple."

## What the user likes

"Images spreading apart to reveal content, sticky scroll, that kind of thing." Editorial motion, not tech-demo motion: offset stacks with staggered movement, opacity layers, a print-informed rhythm. Time as material — the same view swiped through times of day, scroll-based reveals.

## Control for (locked)

- **Motion (character):** composed and editorial — staggered, offset, restrained easing; motion serves reading rhythm, never spectacle
- **Restraint:** few moving systems at once; a static fallback always holds

## Leave open — filled + cycled (variation space)

- **Behavior fork (focus control):** (a) images spreading apart to reveal content; (b) sticky scroll sections; (c) staggered offset-stack reveals; (d) scroll-driven time sequence (the locked-off view through dawn/day/dusk/night, swipe or scroll)
- **Page transitions:** treatment open — cut, wipe, image-carry — nothing recorded yet
- **Where motion concentrates:** heroes only / throughout — open
