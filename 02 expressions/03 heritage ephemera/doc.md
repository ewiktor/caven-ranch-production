# Heritage ephemera — expressions doc

**Version:** V1 · 2026-07-16 · Formed at first board pass, from the reference library commentary (no prior version)
**Type:** brand expression (photography / texture — the family archive as material)

## What it is

The 150-year family archive treated as physical material: etched trench-art shells, a camouflage-painted Brodie helmet, creased mid-century documents with dotted leaders, sepia portraits with arched vignettes, the 1891 Robinson Rosner storefront. It shows up as photographed objects, scanned documents, and archival texture inside layouts. It exists because the strategy calls for history "integrated naturally rather than presented as a heritage exhibit" — the archive as touchable evidence, not a museum wall.

## What the user likes

Age communicated through handling, not styling: "creasing, water damage, and weathered patina communicate age and handling"; oxidation, pitting, flaking paint on the helmet; foxing on the portraits. The expressive folk-art surprise of the trench art — "an expressive folk-art image on a cylindrical historical object." The stoic weight of the military portrait. The "persistence of historical graphics within the architecture" of the 1891 facade.

## Control for (locked)

- **Imagery:** real archival objects and documents, photographed or scanned straight — neutral presentation that lets the artifact carry; monochrome/sepia treatments per the portrait benchmarks
- **Texture:** patina, foxing, creases, water stains, oxidation, film grain — earned wear only, never decorative distressing applied to new things
- **Mood:** personal and historical weight; stoic, quiet, evidentiary

## Leave open — filled + cycled (variation space)

- **Which artifacts (filled):** from `03 media/` and `06 knowledge/` only — the WWI shell and helmet, the political ephemera card, the sepia family portrait, the Robinson photos are already in media; facts about them come from `06 knowledge/family-history.md`, nothing invented
- **Role in a composition (cycled):** hero object on open field / inset evidence inside an editorial layout / background texture / a scanned-document typographic system (dotted leaders, utilitarian sans)
- **Treatment (cycled):** straight object photography, arched-vignette sepia, black-and-white architectural documentation
