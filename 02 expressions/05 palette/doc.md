Every design update picks one at random and builds in it; spread across the run to compare. The A/B (earth/river) framing below is retained as context but is no longer the operative fork.

## What it is

The brand's color world, currently a two-direction fork the user wants tested against each other. **Direction A (earth):** deep terracotta, weathered sage, pale limestone beige — derived from the ranch's physical materials (limestone, cedar, oxidized soils). **Direction B (river):** aquatic blue and deep green from the river experience, against limestone beige and dark wood brown — "a refreshing visual pop against the otherwise arid environment." Both sit on the same low-saturation, limestone-base foundation with a deep charcoal anchor. Color is treated as tactile material — matte ceramic, sun-bleached stone, rusted metal, paper.

## What the user likes

Shared mid-tone value across hues — "the shared mid-tone value creates harmony, while warm ochre and cool slate provide controlled contrast." Restraint in application: "red ochre and sage are used as restrained accents rather than broad fields." Tactility: paper texture, travertine and matte ceramic giving the neutrals depth. The water-derived cools: "desaturated teal and sage, drawn from river water and trees."

## Control for (locked)

- **Color (base, both directions):** limestone beige, off-white cream, warm sand (Indian Khaki register); deep charcoal (Cod Gray register) as the dark anchor
- **Value discipline:** low saturation throughout; accents share the mid-tone value of the base; accents restrained, never broad floods
- **Texture:** color reads through material — paper, travertine, matte ceramic, sun-bleached stone, rusted metal
- **Direction A accents:** deep terracotta **#8B391D** (the user replaced a brighter poppy with this); muted sage **#969582**; terracotta sand **#B78962**; red ochre; deep forest green / muted lime at the evergreen end
- **Direction B accents:** aquatic/river blue, deep water-green, desaturated teal; dusty slate blue **#97A2AD**; dark wood brown

## Scenarios

- **Direction A vs. Direction B** — the user's catalogue says "test both across concepts." Variations of any surface get built per color world and judged as worlds against each other. No build dependency.

## Leave open — filled + cycled (variation space)

- **Which accent leads within a direction (cycled):** terracotta-led warmth / sage / ochre (A); river blue-green / teal / slate (B); pale butter yellow as the vintage-paper register available to both
- **Dark-ground mode (cycled):** deep navy appears in the illustration references (white ink on navy) and indigo in the shibori direction — whether a dark blue joins as a ground is open
