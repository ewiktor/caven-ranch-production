# Feedback — full-frame portal

Commentary of record, verbatim (loaded 2026-07-16). No live band walk-through yet.

From the user's Expressions Catalogue v4 (months old — "use as you will"):

> "The website design prioritizes full bleed and immersive visuals where the homepage acts as a portal using a single frame atmospheric video or image with no initial scroll. Sub pages continue this logic by presenting maps or room tours as immersive screens rather than long scrolling text pages to ensure the user feels transported to the ranch immediately. It follows the minimalist approach of Ett Hem and Monteverdi by letting high quality media dominate the interface while keeping navigation subtle and secondary." — Full-Frame

From the user's Creative Concepts doc (Notion):

> "Ett Hem is a small hotel in Stockholm that won best hotel in the world from Travel and Leisure. Their web art direction is extremely simple. Big illustration up top, then below it is a gallery of images and short video moment vignettes. That is it."

> "The site design for Caven Ranch should start from that level of simplicity and build up from there. Not start complex and trim back. Start simple, then add."
