# Split-screen layout — expressions doc

**Version:** V1 · 2026-07-16 · Split out of the first pass's "editorial layout" at the user's direction to make the layout systems concrete
**Type:** brand expression (layout system)

## What it is

The dual-panel compositional system: a strict 50/50 or weighted split where one side is structured (type, color panel, grid) and the other organic (full-bleed photography, foliage, texture) — and elements deliberately cross the seam to bind the halves. It is one of the brand's concrete layout moves for pages and cards, sitting under the negative-space stance ([04](../04%20negative%20space%20layout/doc.md)).

## What the user likes

The discipline-versus-organic contrast: "a structured graphic panel with an organic full-bleed photograph." The seam-crosser: "a portrait image crosses the central seam, connecting the two halves." Panels in muted color pairs (sage/beige). Ultra-wide imagery emphasizing architectural horizontality on one side of the split.

## Control for (locked)

- **Zones:** two clear panels; one structured, one organic; the split is stated, not blurred
- **Balance:** at least one element crossing the seam, deliberately
- **Grid:** corner navigation, vertical metadata, strict internal margins inside each panel

## Leave open — filled + cycled (variation space)

- **Split ratio (focus control):** 50/50 / 36-64 / weighted — cycle
- **Panel contents:** color panel vs. image; stacked grid vs. single bleed; handwritten vs. set type — cycle
- **Panel colors (filled from [05 palette](../05%20palette/doc.md)):** sage/beige, cream/terracotta, per color direction
