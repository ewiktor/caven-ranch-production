# Feedback — horizontal scroll narrative

Commentary of record, verbatim (loaded 2026-07-16). No live band walk-through yet.

From the user's Creative Concepts doc (Notion):

> "When you click into a specific family story, whether from Marsha's List or from a separate story gallery or page, the story itself becomes a horizontal scroll experience. The page scrolls sideways instead of vertically. Text, photos, graphic elements, illustrations, whatever serves the story."

> "Illustration could play a role in telling these stories. Showing the guy wandering after falling off the horse. Showing the flood. These are moments where illustration might carry more weight and feel more honest than trying to create photorealistic imagery of events from the 1800s and early 1900s."

> "The proving out for now might just be a couple of frames showing what sections of a story would look like with real imagery, real content, and real information, not actually building the horizontal scroll interaction yet."
