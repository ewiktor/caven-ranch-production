**Type:** brand expression (type)

## What it is

The brand's type voices. The old catalogue (months old) names two: a blocky utilitarian sans from the Johnson City sign lineage, and a weathered stamped/typewriter voice from the Walter Caven campaign card — and explicitly avoids "overly delicate or high-contrast fashion serifs." The newer reference library leans hard the other way: high-contrast editorial serifs lead the navigation, layout, and card references, with gestural script as an accent. Both stances are the user's words at different times; the tension is the thing to test.

## What the user likes

From the references: "a high-contrast serif for primary menu items… a structured sans serif for secondary links and metadata"; drop caps, chapter numerals, oversized serif anchors; "a gestural script mark integrated into a serif paragraph." From the catalogue: the utilitarian sign-painting voice, the typewriter register with dotted leaders and structured biographical data — type that feels found on the ranch rather than imported from fashion.

## Control for (locked)

- **Registers (both stances agree):** a small structured secondary register for metadata (tracked caps or typewriter); dramatic scale contrast between display and detail; script only as an accent, never body
- **Provenance rule:** type voices tie to real artifacts of the place — the sign, the campaign card, the archive — not to trend

## Leave open — filled + cycled (variation space)

- **Display voice (focus control — the open tension, build as worlds):** (a) high-contrast editorial serif (the reference library's lean); (b) blocky utilitarian sans (the catalogue's Johnson City sign lean); (c) a pairing where one leads and the other holds structure
- **Accent voice:** gestural script / typewriter-stamp — cycle
- **Numerals and devices:** drop caps, chapter numerals, dotted leaders, plate captions — cycle per surface

## Negative

- **Piazzolla** — killed by the user 2026-07-17, live in session: "do not use that anymore." Not a display face, not a body face, not anywhere.
- (The catalogue's "avoid overly delicate or high-contrast fashion serifs" is months old and contradicted by the newer reference library the user assembled; carried as the open tension below, not as a kill.)
