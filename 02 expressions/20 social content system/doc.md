# Social content system — expressions doc

**Version:** V1 · 2026-07-16 · Formed at second board pass, from the board's Social Content Concept band + the old catalogue's social formats + the user's direction (chat: social media mockups)
**Type:** brand expression (social layout system)

## What it is

The design system for the brand's social surfaces: 4:5 carousels and single-image posts built as recurring, recognizable series rather than one-offs. The old catalogue establishes the series logic — a naturalist field-notes series, an insider's-list local recommendations series, a heritage-fragments found-objects series, a deadpan story-panel series — each with its own fixed format so the feed reads as a publication. The board carries a Social Content Concept band and social sub-band under Layout.

## What the user likes

Series thinking: recurring formats with fixed typographic registers (typewriter voice for field notes, punchy display for pricing/objection pieces). The editorial card grammar carried to social scale: corner-weighted type, plates and mats, archival texture. Deadpan tone where the character or history leads.

## Control for (locked)

- **Format:** 4:5 carousels and single posts; every series has a fixed, repeatable frame system
- **Safezone:** critical content clear of platform chrome (per `logic/controls - composition.md` social controls)
- **Brand:** the animal character appears unannounced when it appears ([01](../01%20coyote%20character/doc.md) presence rule); facts from `06 knowledge/` only

## Leave open — filled + cycled (variation space)

- **Series fork (focus control):** (a) field-notes botanical entries (catalog angle, typewriter register); (b) insider's list (one local place done right, opinionated voice); (c) heritage fragments (found objects, macro + story); (d) story panels (deadpan illustrated family lore); (e) character sightings (unannounced, trail-cam or illustration) — each series is its own variation world
- **Frame system per series:** cycled at exploration
- **Color direction:** rides the A/B scenario in [05 palette](../05%20palette/doc.md)
