## What it is

The cabins as a subject: what they are made of, how they sit on the hillside, and how a person moves through them. The architecture is the thing the $400–500 rate is supposed to be justified by, so this expression exists to carry evidence rather than adjectives — the material palette, the structural idea, and the arrival sequence, shown specifically enough that a skeptical reader can judge build quality for themselves.

The governing condition: **the cabins are in development.** One launches first, with a small number to follow, and low unit count is a permanent operating decision rather than a phase. Nothing here may be framed as operating. This is not a constraint to work around — it is what makes a material-and-drawing register the honest form for the subject, since matter and intent are real now and a finished building is not.

## Filled — determined by knowledge and media, not free to invent

- **The material palette:** vertical cedar plank siding; exposed heavy timber rafters; oxide-red Corten steel beams, slender columns, and pilotis; dry-stack fieldstone masonry and oversized fieldstone slabs; irregular limestone slabs and a limestone-and-gravel courtyard; cast concrete piers, plinth, and patio; large-format glass with thin-profile frames; wire mesh railings and fine steel cable infill; and corrugated metal roofing with deep eaves. — `03 media/timber,-steel,-and-fieldstone-construction.md`, `weathered-timber-and-corten-steel-cabin.md`, `vertical-cedar-and-limestone-hardscaping.md`.
- **The structural idea:** architecturally designed permanent structures cantilevered off a hillside, cedar and limestone, pitched roofs, glass walls. The pitched roof is a deliberate traditional form that keeps the cabin from reading too modern or too spaceship. The cantilever pushes guests forward and out over the landscape in the front living space, then sets them back into the trees in the bedroom where the cabin meets the land. — `06 knowledge/property-details-2.md`.
- **The arrival sequence:** limestone-and-gravel courtyard with native grasses at the entry level; a wall on the road side screening the parking; the courtyard grounding the arrival so a stilted structure is not something you walk up at from below; a ramp down the side where the view opens in front of you before you turn left and enter. — same source. This is the richest narrative material the expression holds.
- **The rooms:** front living space with westward glass; a reading nook carved out and jutting from the main footprint; the bedroom set back into the hillside; a kitchenette, a fire pit, and espresso and coffee machines; open ground under the cabin between the steel beams. — same source.

## Leave open — cycled (variation space)

- **Register (focus control — build as distinct directions, do not average):** (a) **material specimen** — tight crops of matter, the building withheld entirely, the reference's own move; (b) **drawing** — the cantilever and the arrival sequence as a section or plan with materials keyed to it, which is where [07 property cartography](../07%20property%20cartography/doc.md)'s blueprint logic and the leader-line keeper both land; (c) **single surface held** — one material at enormous scale with the spec small, testing how few things the page can hold.
- **What the images are:** the cabin media are renderings rather than photographs of built work, which suits the in-development condition. Whether the page admits that plainly in a caption, or simply shows matter and drawing and never implies a photograph of a finished building, is open. Agent judgment: the second reads more confident and is harder to get wrong.
- **Palette:** cycle per [05 palette](../05%20palette/color-directions.md) — the material palette itself is warm cedar, white limestone, and oxide-red steel, which sits closest to Direction 2 (Rust), but the cool directions set the matter against the ground rather than blending with it. Both worth testing.
- **Where it lives:** the lodgings page primarily; the materials register could also serve a section on the home page.
