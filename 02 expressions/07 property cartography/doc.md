# Property cartography — expressions doc

**Version:** V2 · 2026-07-16 · Amended same-session with the user's Creative Concepts doc and Expressions Catalogue v4: the three map styles (technical / hand-drawn / isometric 3D) as the governing fork, plus the interactive-nodes concept
**Type:** brand expression (graphics — the land itself, mapped)

## What it is

The 1,000 acres rendered as cartography, in three competing styles: a calmed-down USGS-style technical topo (contours, grid coordinates, annotations), a hand-drawn field-sketch map (thin ink lines, negative-space heavy, "a ranger's hand-drawn note"), and an isometric 3D model where "the texture and feel is old and characterful — grain, hand-drawn quality — but the three-dimensionality is modern." It recurs wherever the brand needs to prove the land is real and specific: the property-experience page, arrival guides, printed collateral. The map matters because the property experience is physically spread across the land — cabins centralized in one area, swimming at the river, a safari tent being built in another spot for lunch — and the map ties each moment to where it actually happens.

## What the user likes

The map treated as an object with authority: "authoritative and educational… positioning the property as a place of ecological depth and rugged history rather than just a leisure destination" (technical); "organic and sketched on-site… a warm and neighborly guide" (hand-drawn); "old surface and new dimension" as the tension that gives the 3D version its edge. Relief isolated on textured paper, hairline leaders, serif labels, "information without clutter."

## Control for (locked)

- **Subject:** the actual property geography only — boundary, contours, the Pedernales River bend, Miller Creek, ridge elevation, view corridors, the real experience locations (from the media set and `06 knowledge/`; no generic terrain)
- **Treatment:** characterful, aged surface quality regardless of style — grain, textured paper, hand-drawn warmth; hairline leaders; serif labels; informational elegance over utility styling
- **Color:** desaturated terrain registers within [05 palette](../05%20palette/doc.md) — forest green, ochre; wood/stone/matte-clay textures for the 3D mode

## Leave open — filled + cycled (variation space)

- **Style fork (focus control — the user's three named directions; build as distinct variations):** (a) **technical topo** — USGS language, contour lines, coordinates, calmed for legibility; (b) **hand-drawn minimal** — thin ink field-sketch, negative-space heavy; (c) **isometric 3D** — modern geometry with rustic textures (wood, stone, matte clay), diorama/physical-model quality
- **Interactivity (cycled):** static object / clickable nodes at experience locations that open imagery or information ("the concept and the visual direction should be explored and mocked up" — full resolution explicitly deferred by the user)
- **Source mode (filled from `03 media/`):** satellite relief / contour overlay / site plan / angled aerial — swap per variation
- **Role (cycled):** hero object / editorial inset evidence / wayfinding surface / printed-artifact treatment (bridging [03 heritage ephemera](../03%20heritage%20ephemera/doc.md))
- **Annotation density (cycled):** bare form → light labels → full wayfinding
