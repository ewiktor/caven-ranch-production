## Round 2 — user comments on the exploration (2026-07-16), Group 7
(Group 5 = colors, already covered. Group 6 = lettering, skipped in Figma.)

**7.I — Survey plate (technical topo)**
- **Likes this map style and the callouts** — colors may change, but the callout approach works.
- **The survey-index table is the best table seen so far** (keeper).
- **Likes the "N + arrow" instead of a full compass** — nice, minimal.
- Kills: **the font**; **hates IBM Plex Mono** ("shouldn't use mono anyway"); the line next to the property-survey index; unclear distinction between the italic and the semibold label styles; "River / to Cabins" font not right.

**7.II — Field sketch (hand-drawn, "Defer to the Land")**
- **This map style is potentially really cool** — looks great here; sketch-style, full-bleed. Keep exploring it.
- **NEW KEEPER — a fun/playful way of writing** ("turkeys usually" top-right). That copy voice could be a nice thing to keep exploring (not everywhere).
- **Special Elite works well here.** N+arrow liked again.
- Kills: "Caven Ranch" + generic "same family, 150 years" + "swim here, flat limestone shelves, deep pools" — **that copy needs to be more connected to the actual map** (labels tied to real locations, not boilerplate).

**7.III — Clay diorama (3D)**
- **3D / clay is out** — "not for me… we shouldn't bring it forward like this." **Dislikes the dark background.**
- Kills: the font, IBM Plex Mono, the generic "1,000 acres, same family, 150 years."
- **BUT the callout-with-image idea has potential** (the photo inset for a location, "The River") — if designed better. Kill the "01" numbering, the Archivo font, and the colors.

**Cross-cutting:**
- **Mono is dead** (IBM Plex Mono hated; no mono) — Special Elite stays the lone exception for the right small/sketch contexts.
- **NEW keeper: a playful/fun copy voice** worth developing.
- **Keepers:** map callouts, the survey table, leader lines, "N + arrow" minimal compass, both the technical and sketch map styles.
- **Kills:** 3D/clay, dark backgrounds, the recurring boilerplate copy ("150 years / same family / 1,000 acres"), Archivo, corner "01" numbering.
- Copy should connect to its context, not repeat brand boilerplate.
