# Gallery and carousel — expressions doc

**Version:** V1 · 2026-07-16 · Formed at second board pass, from the user's Creative Concepts doc (Ett Hem carry-forwards) + the gallery/grid references
**Type:** brand expression (web layout system)

## What it is

How the brand presents sets of imagery: galleries, carousels, and vignette strips — named by the user as a core way of presenting imagery (the Ett Hem carry-forward), with the explicit brief to find "a more interesting approach to laying out the carousel and gallery elements than just centering them inside negative space." Includes the moment-vignette idea: short video/photo captures of a view or a thing happening.

## What the user likes

The Ett Hem baseline (gallery under an oversized illustration) as a starting point, not the answer. From the references: overlapping corner-touch grids, illustration-anchor-into-gallery vertical flow, generous space around compact clusters.

## Control for (locked)

- **Imagery:** sets presented with editorial intent — moment vignettes (a view, a thing happening) over posed coverage
- **Space:** clusters held in generous space; the gallery never becomes a wall of thumbnails

## Leave open — filled + cycled (variation space)

- **Layout fork (focus control — the user's explicit brief):** (a) overlapping corner-touch pairs/clusters; (b) offset scattered gallery with scale variance; (c) horizontal vignette strip (rides [12](../12%20horizontal%20scroll%20narrative/doc.md)); (d) illustration-anchored gallery (the Ett Hem shape, made ours) — anything but plain centering
- **Motion:** static / staggered reveal / swipe — rides [09 site motion](../09%20site%20motion/doc.md)
- **Contents (filled):** from `03 media/` — landscape, materials, river, cabins-in-progress honestly framed

## Negative

- **Just centering the gallery inside negative space** — the user's stated thing-to-beat ("a more interesting approach… than just centering them").
