# Weathered Timber and Corten Steel #2

## Description

The cabin's facade is defined by vertical board-and-batten timber siding with a natural, weathered grain that integrates into the surrounding woodland. The hardscaping features a prominent elevated walkway constructed from industrial steel grating and a rusted Corten steel railing system, providing a high-contrast texture against the organic wood. Supporting the structure are dark metal pilotis and a foundation of dry-stack fieldstone, grounding the lightweight architectural elements. The roof assembly utilizes deep timber eaves with exposed rafter tails, emphasizing a material-forward approach to rustic modernism.
