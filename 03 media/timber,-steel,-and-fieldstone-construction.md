# Timber, Steel, and Fieldstone Construction

## Description

The cabin's material palette is defined by vertical cedar siding and exposed heavy timber rafters that create a warm, organic shell. This wood structure is supported by a rigid industrial framework of deep-red oxide steel beams and slender columns, providing a sharp material contrast. The hardscaping utilizes raw, oversized fieldstone slabs for terraced steps and a base foundation of dry-stack stone masonry. Metal mesh railings and large-format glass panes complete the envelope, blending industrial durability with natural textures.
