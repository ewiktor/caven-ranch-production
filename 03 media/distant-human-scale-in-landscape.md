# Distant Human Scale in Landscape

## Description

This composition utilizes a high-angle, wide-shot perspective to establish the vast scale of a property's natural environment, placing human subjects as small anchors within the frame. The framing follows the organic S-curve of the river, leading the eye from the foreground limestone shelves toward the distant horizon to create a sense of depth and exploration. By positioning the subjects in the middle ground rather than the foreground, the concept emphasizes the relationship between the inhabitant and the expansive landscape rather than specific architectural details. The vertical orientation and high horizon line prioritize the ground plane, showcasing the property's unique topography and spatial flow.
