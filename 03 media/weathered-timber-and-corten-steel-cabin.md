# Weathered Timber and Corten Steel Cabin

## Description

The cabin's facade is defined by vertical raw timber planks with visible grain and natural weathering, creating a rhythmic, organic texture. This wood cladding is paired with a structural hardscaping system featuring a rusted Corten steel ramp and railing that provides a sharp, industrial contrast to the soft forest floor. The foundation utilizes cast-in-place concrete piers and a central concrete plinth, elevating the structure to minimize site impact. Corrugated metal roofing with deep eaves protects the timber walls, while the ramp's fine steel cable infill maintains a lightweight, permeable aesthetic for the hardscaping.
