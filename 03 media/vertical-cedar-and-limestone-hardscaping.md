# Vertical Cedar and Limestone Hardscaping

## Description

The cabin's exterior is defined by vertical cedar plank siding that provides a warm, rhythmic texture against the surrounding greenery. The structural foundation utilizes a combination of rough-hewn fieldstone masonry for the lower level and slender steel pilotis anchored in concrete footings. The hardscaping features a rugged, tiered pathway constructed from large, irregular limestone slabs that transition into a smooth concrete patio. Metal elements, including the thin-profile window frames and wire mesh railings, provide a sharp, industrial contrast to the organic wood and stone surfaces.
