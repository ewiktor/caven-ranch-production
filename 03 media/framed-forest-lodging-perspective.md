# Framed Forest Lodging Perspective

## Description

This composition utilizes a natural canopy of oak trees to frame the lodging, creating a sense of discovery and seclusion. The camera is positioned at a medium distance across a paved threshold, using the foreground road as a grounding element that leads the eye toward the elevated structure. The framing emphasizes the relationship between the architecture and the environment, specifically how the building is nestled within the verticality of the trees rather than dominating the landscape. Soft, diffused sunlight filters through the leaves, creating a dappled light effect that prioritizes the atmospheric mood of the site over architectural detail.
