# Limestone Riverbed and Cerulean Water

## Description

This reference highlights the geological interplay between weathered limestone formations and clear, multi-tonal river water. The rock structures feature heavy erosion with rounded edges and deep undercuts, showcasing a transition from light tan surfaces to dark, pitted gray textures. The water exhibits a distinct color gradient, shifting from a pale, translucent teal in the shallow pools to a vibrant cerulean blue in the deeper channels. The composition emphasizes the natural layering of the stone shelves as they descend into the water, creating a rugged and serene property setting.
