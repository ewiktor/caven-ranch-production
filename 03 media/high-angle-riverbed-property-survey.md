# High-Angle Riverbed Property Survey

## Description

This composition utilizes a high-angle aerial perspective to establish the scale and geographical context of a property's natural boundaries. The framing employs a diagonal leading line created by the riverbed, which draws the eye from the foreground deep into the horizon, emphasizing the vastness of the land. By positioning the camera to capture the intersection of dense forest and rocky water features, the shot focuses on the conceptual relationship between different terrain types rather than specific textures. The deep depth of field ensures that the entire landscape remains in focus, providing a comprehensive visual map of the property's environmental assets.
