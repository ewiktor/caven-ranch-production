# Hilly Scrubland and Oak Savanna

## Description

This reference showcases a dense property setting characterized by rolling hills covered in a mix of live oaks and juniper-style scrub trees. The foreground features mature, gnarled trees with textured bark and small, waxy leaves, while the mid-ground transitions into a hazy, layered canopy of Mediterranean-style vegetation. Atmospheric perspective is used to emphasize the depth of the property, with the distant hills fading into a soft blue-grey mist.
