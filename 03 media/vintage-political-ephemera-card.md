# Vintage Political Ephemera Card

## Description

This reference showcases a mid-century family ephemera item, specifically a political campaign card, characterized by its heavy physical distress and utilitarian typography. The subject matter is grounded in personal history, featuring a halftone-printed portrait and sans-serif type that has been weathered by time, moisture, and folding. The paper substrate exhibits significant creasing, foxing, and water staining, which adds a layer of tactile history to the graphic layout. The composition uses a balanced, centered typographic hierarchy typical of mid-20th-century local printing, emphasizing the candidate's name and qualifications.
