# Etched WWI Trench Art Shell

## Description

This reference showcases a World War I artillery shell repurposed as trench art, featuring a hand-etched illustration of a cavalry soldier. The graphic subject matter is defined by thin, precise line work scratched into the dark, patinated brass surface, creating a high-contrast metallic outline. The illustration style is folk-art inspired, utilizing primitive yet expressive contour lines to depict the soldier's uniform, Brodie helmet, and the horse's harness. The cylindrical form of the shell provides a unique curved canvas, where the dark oxidation of the metal serves as a negative space background for the bright, incised graphics.
