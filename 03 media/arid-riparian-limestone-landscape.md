# Arid Riparian Limestone Landscape

## Description

This reference highlights a natural property setting characterized by large, weathered limestone slabs and boulders that transition into a shallow water feature. The vegetation is defined by a mix of dense evergreen scrub, prickly pear cacti, and deciduous trees with vibrant green canopies, creating a layered xeric environment. The water exhibits a distinct olive-green to murky teal hue, reflecting the surrounding silt and limestone bed rather than a clear blue. The geological focus is on the flat, sedimentary rock shelves and the angular, stacked boulders that provide structural focal points within the riparian zone.
