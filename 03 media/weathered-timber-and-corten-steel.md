# Weathered Timber and Corten Steel

## Description

The cabin's exterior is defined by vertical board-and-batten timber siding with a natural, matte finish that emphasizes raw wood grain. This organic texture is contrasted by the industrial hardscaping of the elevated walkway and staircase, which are constructed from heavily oxidized Corten steel featuring a rich, rust-orange patina. Beneath the structure, the ground-level hardscaping utilizes light-toned, rough-hewn stone pavers and stacked stone retaining walls to ground the architectural palette. The interplay between the warm, tactile wood and the weathered, metallic surfaces creates a cohesive material language of durability and natural aging.
