# Aerial River Meander Composition

## Description

This reference establishes a high-angle aerial concept for property documentation, using a winding river as a natural leading line to create a sense of scale and boundary. The composition utilizes a diagonal flow that draws the eye from the foreground into the distant horizon, emphasizing the vastness of the landscape rather than specific ground details. The framing captures the intersection of water, rock, and dense vegetation, providing a conceptual overview of the property's environmental context and topographical layout. By positioning the horizon line in the upper third, the shot prioritizes the spatial relationship between the natural features and the overall land mass.
