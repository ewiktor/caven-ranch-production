# The drive in

**Goal** — Convert "remote" from a fear into the product itself — the approach as designed experience; answers the access/practicality objection.

**Move** — The considering visitor, from "trusts it" toward "ready to act."

**Person & want** — Fear & risk: will a remote property be inconvenient? Experiential: the unwinding they came for. Security: knowing exactly how arrival works.

**Message** — Fifty minutes from Austin, the road narrows, the trees close in, and the view is withheld until you step out at your cabin — the arrival is the first amenity.

**Angle** — Narrative, observational — told as the drive itself.

**Execution** — Web section or horizontal sequence per [12 horizontal scroll narrative](../02%20expressions/12%20horizontal%20scroll%20narrative/doc.md) (the arrival-drive fork); could also be the lodgings page's opening. Landscape and road media; the withheld-view beat is the structure.

**Draws on** — `06 knowledge/guest-experience-2.md`: the ~50-minute drive, four-lane to two-lane winding backroad, the panhandle drive, first gate, the westward views hidden by the tree line, the 100-foot drop, the Ashe Juniper tunnel, the view opening only at the cabin ramp.
