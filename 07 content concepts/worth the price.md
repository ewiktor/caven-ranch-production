# Worth the price

**Goal** — Answer the price objection head-on with evidence; serves the decision stage (the strategy: establish why $400–500/night is justified).

**Move** — The considering visitor, from "trusts it" to "ready to act."

**Person & want** — Practical + security: what exactly does the money buy? Fear: paying premium for staged luxury. Competence: making a defensible choice.

**Message** — The price buys build quality, privacy, space, and uncommon access to preserved land — not resort infrastructure.

**Angle** — Expository + comparison (against hotels and conventional rentals, without naming targets).

**Execution** — Social carousel (6 frames, photo-dominant, punchy copy) per [20 social content system](../02%20expressions/20%20social%20content%20system/doc.md); a web FAQ/decision section sibling. Materials macro imagery; the map as evidence ([07](../02%20expressions/07%20property%20cartography/doc.md)).

**Draws on** — `06 knowledge/guest-experience-2.md` ($400–500 range, self-directed, no conventional infrastructure), `property-details-2.md` (build specifics). Use the range, not a single number; nothing framed as bookable yet.
