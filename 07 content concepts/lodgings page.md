# Lodgings page

**Goal** — Let a prospective guest imagine the stay — cabins, comfort, privacy — honestly framed as in development; serves the imagine-the-stay stage of the strategy.

**Move** — The interested visitor, from "knows it and feels something" to "trusts it."

**Person & want** — Practical: will a remote property be comfortable and well considered? Fear: basic accommodations presented as exclusive. Live wants: security (evidence over adjectives) and competence (understanding exactly what they'd get).

**Message** — Architecturally distinctive cabins pair rugged surroundings with considered comfort — built to defer to the land, not dominate it.

**Angle** — Expository + demonstration: floor plans, materials, and specifics doing the persuading.

**Execution** — Web page. Materials and construction imagery from `03 media/` (timber/Corten/limestone set); floor-plan-as-blueprint treatment (the old catalogue's technical-annotations idea) under [07 property cartography](../02%20expressions/07%20property%20cartography/doc.md)'s style logic; split-screen and framing systems ([10](../02%20expressions/10%20split-screen%20layout/doc.md), [18](../02%20expressions/18%20image%20framing/doc.md)).

**Draws on** — `06 knowledge/property-details-2.md`, `guest-experience-2.md`; `03 media/` cabin/material docs. Hard rule pressure point: cabins are planned/in progress — nothing framed as operating.
