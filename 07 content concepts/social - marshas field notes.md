# Social — Marsha's field notes

**Goal** — A recurring series that proves ecological depth one specimen at a time; serves credibility and steady feed presence.

**Move** — The follower, from "knows it but feels nothing" to "knows it and feels something."

**Person & want** — Values: conservation, real knowledge. Curiosity: the pleasure of a well-made catalog entry.

**Message** — Someone here knows this land species by species.

**Angle** — Catalog: one specimen per entry, named, classified, placed.

**Execution** — Recurring social carousel series (4 frames, 4:5) per [20 social content system](../02%20expressions/20%20social%20content%20system/doc.md): botanical/fauna illustration ([02](../02%20expressions/02%20illustration%20language/doc.md), the shibori/embroidered direction is tied to this content) + one real photo frame + typewriter-register notes ([21 typography](../02%20expressions/21%20typography/doc.md)). Feeds and is fed by [13 infinite canvas](../02%20expressions/13%20infinite%20canvas/doc.md).

**Draws on** — `06 knowledge/marsha's-list-2.md` (Guadalupe bass, bald cypress, golden-cheeked warbler, wildflower record); entries only for species actually in the record.
