# Dawn to dark

**Goal** — Sell the rhythm of a day on the land — light as the amenity; serves imagine-the-stay.

**Move** — The interested visitor, from "knows it and feels something" to "trusts it."

**Person & want** — Experiential: what a day there actually feels like. Emotional: restoration, natural wonder (sunsets, stargazing are in the offer).

**Message** — Same view, same chair — a different world every few hours.

**Angle** — Observational; timestamps as the only copy.

**Execution** — Web section: the same locked-off view at dawn / midday / golden hour / night as a horizontal swipe strip per [12 horizontal scroll narrative](../02%20expressions/12%20horizontal%20scroll%20narrative/doc.md) (time-of-day fork) and [09 site motion](../02%20expressions/09%20site%20motion/doc.md). Four equal panels, minimal chrome.

**Draws on** — `06 knowledge/` (sunsets, stargazing, the westward views); `03 media/` landscape set — the locked-off view itself is a to-make asset (no four-time sequence exists yet).
