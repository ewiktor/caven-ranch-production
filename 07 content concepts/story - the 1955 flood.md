# Story — The 1955 flood

**Goal** — Show the family's bond with a land that is genuinely powerful — respect, not postcard; serves credible-story depth and the land-as-protagonist message.

**Move** — The curious visitor, from "knows it and feels something" to "trusts it."

**Person & want** — Emotional: awe with weight. Values: truth about a real place over staged charm.

**Message** — The river that makes this place also tests it — and the family stayed.

**Angle** — Narrative, plain-spoken.

**Execution** — Horizontal-scroll story per [12 horizontal scroll narrative](../02%20expressions/12%20horizontal%20scroll%20narrative/doc.md); illustration carries the night-flood moments per the user's honesty argument; the still-visible house foundation is the archival anchor ([03 heritage ephemera](../02%20expressions/03%20heritage%20ephemera/doc.md)).

**Draws on** — `06 knowledge/family-history.md`: the 1955 Pedernales flood — Ralph and Esther swept from their car at night, separated, each surviving in trees, the dog too; the house foundation still visible at a shocking distance from the river.
