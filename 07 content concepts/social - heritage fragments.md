# Social — Heritage fragments

**Goal** — A recurring found-objects series that carries the 150 years through physical things; serves credible-story depth.

**Move** — The follower, from "knows it but feels nothing" to "knows it and feels something."

**Person & want** — Emotional: objects with weight. Curiosity: what is that thing?

**Message** — This history is physical — you could hold it.

**Angle** — Archival: one artifact per entry, its story told plainly.

**Execution** — Recurring series (poster-ratio singles or short carousels) per [20 social content system](../02%20expressions/20%20social%20content%20system/doc.md): macro artifact hero + typewriter story text per [03 heritage ephemera](../02%20expressions/03%20heritage%20ephemera/doc.md). First entries: the engraved shell, the Brodie helmet, the campaign card, the sepia portraits.

**Draws on** — `06 knowledge/family-history.md`; `03 media/` artifact set. Only artifacts that exist in media/knowledge; stories verified against knowledge at compile.
