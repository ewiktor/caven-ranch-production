# Home page

**Goal** — Introduce Caven Ranch and make its difference legible in one screen; serves the big goal of qualified guests seeing enough value to book when booking exists.

**Move** — The design-conscious Austin/San Antonio traveler, from "doesn't know it exists" to "knows it and feels something."

**Person & want** — Experiential: short escapes from work, screens, crowds. Values: privacy, architecture, credible story. Fear: gimmicky lodging, staged luxury. Live wants: curiosity (what is this place?) and identity (brands that do something unexpected).

**Message** — A 1,000-acre family ranch on the Pedernales, held 150 years, is becoming a small, design-led retreat — and it is nothing like the Hill Country lodging you already know.

**Angle** — Poetic + observational: the land and the strangeness of the brand carry it; no hard claims.

**Execution** — Web page (the portal). Hero per [14 full-frame portal](../02%20expressions/14%20full-frame%20portal/doc.md) or [04 negative space layout](../02%20expressions/04%20negative%20space%20layout/doc.md) (two worlds to test); oversized illustration option per [02](../02%20expressions/02%20illustration%20language/doc.md); gallery below per [17](../02%20expressions/17%20gallery%20and%20carousel/doc.md); the character may appear unannounced ([01](../02%20expressions/01%20coyote%20character/doc.md)).

**Draws on** — `06 knowledge/property-details-2.md`, `family-history.md` (the one-line facts); `03 media/` aerials and landscape set. No booking claims — the retreat is in development.
