# Site preloader

**Goal** — Make first contact feel authored and strange-in-a-good-way before a single word is read; serves brand distinctiveness.

**Move** — Any first-time visitor, from "doesn't know it exists" to "knows it and feels something" — in three seconds.

**Person & want** — The Austin creative-class visitor; identity and curiosity — drawn to brands that do something unexpected.

**Message** — (Wordless.) Something lives here, and this brand has a sense of humor it isn't going to explain.

**Angle** — Observational, deadpan.

**Execution** — The once-only home-page load moment per [08 preloader](../02%20expressions/08%20preloader/doc.md): the animal walks in and lies down in the right corner, script-mixed type opposite ([01 coyote character](../02%20expressions/01%20coyote%20character/doc.md) presence rule: never explained). Static fallback frame required.

**Draws on** — `03 media/` none required (illustration-led); knowledge only for nothing — no facts asserted.
