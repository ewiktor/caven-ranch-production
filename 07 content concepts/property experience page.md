# Property experience page

**Goal** — Communicate that the experience is spread across 1,000 real acres — moments tied to actual places; serves imagine-the-stay and worth-the-price.

**Move** — The interested visitor, from "knows it and feels something" to "trusts it."

**Person & want** — Experiential: nature without giving up comfort. Curiosity: what would I actually do there? Autonomy: self-directed stays ("no programming, no staff managing guest time" — knowledge).

**Message** — Your stay happens across the land — the river, the overlooks, the outpost, the fireside — and the map is real.

**Angle** — Demonstration via the map; observational vignettes per location.

**Execution** — Web page anchored by the interactive property map per [07 property cartography](../02%20expressions/07%20property%20cartography/doc.md) (style worlds: technical / hand-drawn / isometric 3D; clickable nodes opening imagery). Mock the concept and visual direction; full interaction explicitly deferred by the user.
