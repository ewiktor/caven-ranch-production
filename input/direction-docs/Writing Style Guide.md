## Voice Attributes

The Caven Ranch voice is like a trusted Hill Country neighbor sharing moments from their day and old stories over a sunset glass of wine: warm, intelligent, and subtly humorous.

### 1. Authentic and Warm

- This voice feels genuine and welcoming, like a multi-generational family ranch hand extending effortless hospitality. It grounds communications in real stories and sensory details from the land, fostering a sense of belonging and amplified contentment without forcing "Texas stereotypes" like howling wolves or longhorns.
- Use contractions, first-person anecdotes (e.g., "We've watched the sunsets here for generations"), and sensory language (e.g., "the sky opening wide as the heat breaks"). Avoid hype; focus on subtle, neighborly sharing.
- Example: "Step through our gate and feel the day's tension melt away under that impossibly big Hill Country sky—it's the kind of relief we've cherished here for 150 years."

### 2. Sophisticated yet Unserious

- The voice balances intelligence and depth (e.g., ecological insights or historical oddities) with lighthearted ease, never veering into goofiness or pretension.
- Incorporate subtle humor through wry observations or self-deprecating nods; pair factual depth (e.g., master naturalist knowledge) with casual phrasing. Keep sentences varied but rhythmic, avoiding overly formal or salesy tones.
- Example: "The first starry night here will ruin city skies for you - in the best way possible."

### 3. Generous and Insider

- This voice shares knowledge openly, like sketching a trail map for a friend, providing "word-of-mouth" insights without gatekeeping.
- Use inclusive language (e.g., "Here's what you might discover..."); offer practical, story-driven tips (e.g., itineraries as "our perfect day"). Emphasize authenticity over promotion, weaving in real guest anecdotes or ecological facts.
- "We've mapped out the hidden overlook where the wildflowers peak in spring—grab a coffee at dawn and let the morning light get you out of bed and start to open up your day."

## Vocabulary Guidelines

### Words/Phrases We Avoid

- "Rustic charm"
- "Luxury getaway"
- "Instagram-worthy"
- "Wild West adventure"
- "Exclusive retreat"

## Sentence Structure & Rhythm

### Sentence Length

Never write short staccato sentences. Always state thoughts fully.

### Rhythm & Flow

Writing should feel expansive and rhythmic. 

### Punctuation Preferences

Never use em dashes.

## Formatting Conventions

### Capitalization

Capitalize proper nouns (e.g., Hill Country, Caven Ranch); lowercase general terms (e.g., dusk swim, golden hour). Special terms like "Master Naturalist" only if titular.

### Numbers

Spell out 1-10 (e.g., "five cabins"); use numerals for 11+ or dates (e.g., "150-year history," "3 PM check-in").

### DON'T

- Use hype or salesy language (off-brand pretense): Example (don't): "The ultimate luxury escape awaits!" Instead: "Join us for that quiet Hill Country magic."
- Rely on stereotypes (off-brand inauthenticity): Example (don't): "Saddle up for Wild West fun!" Instead: "Wander paths our family has known for generations."
- Overexplain (off-brand clutter): Example (don't): "Our cabins feature limestone walls, cedar accents, and expansive views because..." Instead: "Limestone walls meet cedar warmth—simple, like the land."

## Before/After Examples

### Scenario: Social Media Post About Wildflowers

❌ Before (Off-Brand): "Check out these amazing wildflowers at our exclusive ranch! They're super colorful and perfect for Instagram pics. Don't miss this rustic charm—book now for the best adventure!" 

✅ After (On-Brand): "Spring's first bluebonnets are peeking through the limestone. We've marked our favorite clearing on the trail map; wander out at dusk for the best light."
