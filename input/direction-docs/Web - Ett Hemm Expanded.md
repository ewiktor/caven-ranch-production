The starting point for the website direction is Ett Hem, a small hotel in Stockholm. The site is extremely simple. Each page has one illustration at the top, slightly animated, then a small carousel of imagery below, and static vignette videos that each focus on one captured moment. Not much text. The photography and the moments do the communicating.

The client was shown this and loved it. The appeal is that the property and the design are good enough to speak for themselves without having to explain every detail. Less text, more visual and experiential. That's a lock as a starting point.

The idea is to build from that level of simplicity and add complexity back in specific ways, mainly through motion and through the content concepts that exist for this brand.

### Motion

The site should have more motion and interaction than Ett Hem. There are web motion references in the reference bank that show the territory being explored. Types of motion that have come up:

- A preloader animation when you first land on the site. One idea is the coyote character walking into frame. Another is images spreading apart to reveal the page.
- Sticky scroll, where you land on a page, an image is spread across, and then as you scroll you enter a narrative flow where elements move at different speeds.
- There was some thinking about pages living within a single fixed aspect ratio frame where you don't scroll down traditionally, but that idea partially faded as other concepts developed. It may still be relevant for specific sections.

The client called out that video is the best way to sell a hotel, but every hotel now does one big full-frame video as the hero section. The ask is for a variant, where video is still used but framed or presented in a way that doesn't feel like the default.

### Site Content

The types of content that have been discussed for the site, though how they break into pages or sections is still open:

- General property information
- The cabins and lodgings, presented in some way that feels unique rather than standard hotel room pages
- The property experience, which could tie into the isometric 3D map concept (separate Pottery doc)
- Family history storytelling, possibly using horizontal scroll (separate Pottery doc)
- Nature and conservation content, possibly through the Marsha's List concept (separate Pottery doc)

Whether these are distinct pages, sections within a larger experience, or navigated from a central canvas is not settled. There was a bolder idea about making the entire site navigable from a canvas-like environment rather than a traditional page structure.

### What Carries Over from Ett Hem

The restraint. The minimal text. Illustration as a primary element on pages. Small carousels of imagery. Static vignette videos capturing single moments. The site should feel understated rather than trying to overwhelm with content. The quality and treatment of what's there does the work, not the volume.
