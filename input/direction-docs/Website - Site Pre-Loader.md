A distinct animated moment that plays when you first land on the site. It plays once. It's the first thing you experience before the page content appears.

The reason this matters is that the client specifically wanted something for the entry moment. Every hotel site either loads straight into a static page or plays a big full-frame hero video. Both are the default now. The preloader is a way to do something different for that first second or two, something that has character and sets a tone before you even see the content.

### Ideas on the Table

**Coyote walking into frame:** The brand character (coyote) as a graphic or illustration walks into the frame and lays down in one corner. Script text or type is jammed into the opposite corner. This ties the preloader directly to the brand character concept, where the coyote shows up across the brand in different forms without explanation. Seeing it first thing when you land on the site is one of those appearances. There was a rough exploration of this in Figma/mid-journey where the coyote comes in and lays down and graphics mix into the type in the corners.

**Images spreading apart:** Two images start together covering the frame and slide apart to reveal the page content underneath. More of a motion transition than a character moment. Simpler, less conceptual, but still gives the entry a designed feel rather than just loading in.

### What It Transitions Into

The preloader leads into whatever the homepage is. One layout direction in the references shows a big illustration or graphic above the fold as the primary visual anchor, then you scroll down into a clean gallery or carousel in negative space below. If the preloader involves the coyote illustration, it could transition naturally into that kind of structure where the illustration stays as the above-the-fold element and the page builds below it.

### Separate from Site-Wide Motion

The preloader is a single specific moment that plays once. It is separate from the broader site-wide motion direction (sticky scroll, parallax, staggered transitions between elements, horizontal scroll for stories) which is about how the site behaves as you navigate through it. Those are different things. The preloader is the entrance. The site-wide motion is the ongoing experience.
