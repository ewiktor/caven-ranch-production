# Design standards

When you design anything, you have to pay extra attention to:

- **Typography** — selection, kerning, tracking, size, weight, line-height, pairings, casing, hierarchy between levels
- **Spacing** — spacing scale, grouping/proximity, relative ratios between gaps, padding, margins, alignment to a rhythm, placement of empty space
- **Colors** — selection, pairings, number of colors, accent placement, derived neutrals/tints, contrast, temperature consistency
- **Composition & layout** — focal point, eye path, visual weight/balance, scale contrast, grid, alignment, grouping, negative space as an element, tension/asymmetry
- **Graphic elements & detailing** — lines, borders, dividers, icons, labels/chrome: what earns its place vs decoration, stroke weights, corner radii
- **Hierarchy & emphasis** — what reads first/second/third, how strongly, and through which lever: size vs weight vs color vs position
- **Content-to-form fit** — does the layout choice actually express what the content means, or is it a template with words poured in
- **Craft finishing** — optical alignment, baseline alignment, consistent detail sizing: the last 5% polish pass
