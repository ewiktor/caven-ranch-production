# Settled — off the consideration table

Decisions here feed run prompts as constraints, never as considerations. Only the principal settles something; agents propose, flag, and record.

Each entry carries: the decision, the date, its provenance (the principal's words, a board pass, an existing identity system), and its strength — settled outright, or near-locked pending the principal's confirm. Near-locked items say exactly what is still open about them.

Nothing goes here to make the doc feel complete. An empty section means the thing is genuinely open — that is information, not a gap.
