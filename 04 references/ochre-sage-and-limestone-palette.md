# Ochre Sage and Limestone Palette

## Description

This reference establishes a color hierarchy where neutral limestone, cream, and beige serve as the primary foundation, preventing the palette from feeling overly saturated. The Red Ochre and Sage Green are utilized as concentrated accent points rather than expansive background washes, maintaining a sophisticated balance. Textural elements like pitted travertine and matte ceramics provide organic depth to the cream and beige tones, ensuring the neutral base feels intentional and rich. The overall composition demonstrates how to integrate earthy pigments without overwhelming the visual field with high-chroma color.

## User context

Limestone, cream, and beige form the primary foundation. Red ochre and sage are used as restrained accents rather than broad fields. Travertine and matte ceramic textures give the neutral base depth.
