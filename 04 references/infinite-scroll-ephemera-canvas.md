# Infinite Scroll Ephemera Canvas

## Description

This layout utilizes an expansive, non-linear grid that mimics a physical mood board or digital 'infinite canvas' where assets are scattered organically. The design prioritizes a sense of discovery, using varying image scales and overlapping layers to create depth within a flat UI environment. Central typography acts as an anchor, while the surrounding 'ephemera'—a mix of photography, product shots, and graphics—suggests a clickable, interactive ecosystem. The use of generous negative space between clusters prevents the dense collection of items from feeling cluttered, facilitating a seamless transition from browsing to detailed viewing.
