# Split-Screen Dual Brand Identity Layout

## Description

This layout utilizes a vertical 50/50 split to juxtapose two distinct brand personalities: a structured, 'clean' graphic side and an 'organic' full-bleed photographic side. The left panel features a high-contrast terracotta background with disciplined typography and a centered inset image, representing contemporary clarity. In contrast, the right panel breaks the rigid grid with a full-bleed lifestyle photograph where foreground foliage creates a 'funky', unrefined, and human-centric texture. This composition successfully blends architectural precision with rooted, organic character through the tension of the center seam.
