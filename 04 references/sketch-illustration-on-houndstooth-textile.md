# Sketch Illustration on Houndstooth Textile

## Description

This illustration style utilizes rapid, gestural ink line work to define form through varying stroke weights and energetic hatching. The art direction juxtaposes these loose, hand-drawn equestrian figures against a rigid, micro-houndstooth woven background, creating a sophisticated contrast between organic movement and geometric order. Shading is achieved through dense cross-hatching and flat blocks of earthy brown pigment that allow the underlying textile texture to bleed through. The level of detail is intentionally selective, using minimal linework for the background elements while focusing intricate, scratchy textures on the central jockey and horse figures.
