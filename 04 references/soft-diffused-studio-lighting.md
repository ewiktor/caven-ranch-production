# Soft Diffused Studio Lighting

## Description

The lighting features a soft, directional key light from the upper right that creates a gentle gradient across the textured wall and subject. This high-key approach utilizes a large diffusion source to minimize harsh shadows, resulting in a subtle fall-off that emphasizes the subject's form without high contrast. The neutral, warm-toned illumination bridges the subject and background, creating a cohesive, painterly atmosphere.
