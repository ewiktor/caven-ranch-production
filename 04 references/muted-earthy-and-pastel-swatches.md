# Muted Earthy and Pastel Swatches

## Description

This reference features a sophisticated color story defined by desaturated, earthy tones and soft pastels including dusty slate blue, terracotta clay, and pale butter yellow. The palette balances warm neutrals like cream and sage with a deep charcoal anchor, creating a harmonious, organic feel. These specific color blocks provide a foundation for a brand identity that feels grounded yet airy.
