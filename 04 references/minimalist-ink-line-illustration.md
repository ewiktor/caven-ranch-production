# Minimalist Ink Line Illustration

## Description

This illustration utilizes a gestural, loose ink line style that prioritizes movement and character over anatomical precision. The linework features varying weights, with quick, expressive strokes and intentional over-sketching that creates a sense of spontaneity. Shading is achieved through delicate cross-hatching and dense ink clusters, providing depth to the small-scale figures against a vast, textured paper background. The high level of detail is concentrated in the character interactions, using a minimalist approach to negative space that emphasizes the narrative art direction.

## User context
