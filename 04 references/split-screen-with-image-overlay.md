# Split Screen with Image Overlay

## Description

This layout utilizes a strict 50/50 vertical split composition, featuring a minimalist off-white canvas on the left and a full-bleed landscape photograph on the right. A secondary portrait frame is strategically overlaid across the central axis, breaking the vertical divide and creating a focal point that bridges the two halves. The composition is anchored by a clean typographic grid, with navigation and product details placed at the extreme corners and along the central seam to maintain a high-fashion editorial aesthetic. This layered approach creates depth by stacking a smaller, intimate subject over a vast, expansive background while maintaining a structured, asymmetrical balance.
