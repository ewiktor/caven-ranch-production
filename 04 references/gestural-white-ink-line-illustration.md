# Gestural White Ink Line Illustration

## Description

This illustration style utilizes a high-contrast, gestural line work approach where white pigment is applied over a deep navy textured background. The linework is characterized by varying pressure and speed, creating a raw, sketch-like quality with visible dry-brush effects and intentional imperfections. Shading is achieved through line density and the layering of translucent white strokes rather than traditional gradients, preserving the tactile feel of the canvas substrate. The detail level is minimalist yet expressive, focusing on the essential silhouette and typographic elements to convey form and character.
