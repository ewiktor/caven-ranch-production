# Stamped Fabric Gyotaku Illustration

## Description

This reference showcases a tactile, stamped illustration style where the ink bleeds into a heavy-weave fabric substrate rather than sitting on paper. The composition utilizes a unique split-frame layout where the subject is bisected, placing the tail section at the top and the head section at the bottom to create a continuous wrap-around effect. The typography is intentionally imperfect, featuring slight distortions and ink-spread that follow the natural ripples and texture of the textile surface.
