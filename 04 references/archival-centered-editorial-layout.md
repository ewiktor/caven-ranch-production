# Archival Centered Editorial Layout

## Description

This layout features a sophisticated central alignment that anchors a series of vintage-style photographs and a hand-drawn house illustration. The design utilizes a hierarchy of varied text styles, including delicate script accents and typewriter-inspired serifs, to create an archival storytelling aesthetic. Generous negative space and intentional text tracking provide a clean, breathable structure for historical or brand narrative content.
