# Terracotta and Evergreen Earth Tones

## Description

This palette establishes a grounded, organic foundation by pairing deep forest greens and muted limes with soft neutrals. The user's directive to swap the vibrant Poppy for a deep terracotta (#8B391D) shifts the energy from high-contrast floral to a sophisticated, earthy warmth that complements the Ivory and Creamsicle base. The subtle paper texture across the swatches adds a tactile, premium feel to the color relationships.
