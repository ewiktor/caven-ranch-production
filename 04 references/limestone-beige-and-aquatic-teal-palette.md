# Limestone Beige and Aquatic Teal Palette

## Description

This reference establishes a color strategy where organic limestone beige and off-white cream serve as the primary foundation, avoiding saturated background fills. The secondary accent colors are derived directly from the environment, specifically the desaturated teal and sage green found in the river water and surrounding tree canopy. This creates a sophisticated, earthy balance where the 'Blue Green Water' tones act as natural highlights against a neutral, high-key backdrop. The interplay between the warm stone textures and the cool, muted aquatic hues provides a grounded yet refreshing brand identity.
