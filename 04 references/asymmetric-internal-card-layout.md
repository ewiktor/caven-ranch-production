# Asymmetric Internal Card Layout

## Description

The internal layout of each card utilizes a sophisticated tension between a top-aligned typographic block and a bottom-right anchored visual element. The typography is organized in a strict, left-aligned hierarchy at the top, leaving a significant expanse of negative space in the center to emphasize the verticality of the card. A small, square photographic inset is positioned with a white border in the lower-right quadrant, creating a diagonal visual flow from the text to the image. This specific arrangement uses the 'rule of thirds' internally to balance dense information against a singular, framed focal point.

## User context

A top-aligned typographic block is balanced by a small, bordered square image in the lower-right quadrant. The large central negative space and diagonal relationship between text and image create a controlled vertical composition.
