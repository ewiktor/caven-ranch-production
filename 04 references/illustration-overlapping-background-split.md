# Illustration Overlapping Background Split

## Description

This layout utilizes a central colored block where the illustrative elements act as a bridge, physically cutting across the boundary between the solid green fill and the cream outer frame. The technique creates a sense of depth by having the subject matter exist in both spatial planes simultaneously, effectively 'breaking' the container. This approach is particularly effective for split-screen compositions where a single graphic element can unify two distinct color fields through overlapping transparency or precise masking.
