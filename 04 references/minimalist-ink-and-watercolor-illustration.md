# Minimalist Ink and Watercolor Illustration

## Description

This illustration utilizes a high-contrast ink-on-watercolor technique, where the subject is defined by loose, expressive line work and dense cross-hatching. The art direction favors a minimalist, folk-art aesthetic, using a textured paper substrate that allows the watercolor pigments to pool and create organic, mottled gradients in the background. Shading is achieved through varying line density rather than tonal shifts in the ink, creating a stark graphic quality against the soft, atmospheric wash of the environment. The detail level is intentionally restrained, focusing on the silhouette and rhythmic strokes to convey movement and form without over-rendering.

## User context

Loose black ink and dense cross-hatching define the subject over a mottled watercolor wash. Textured paper, pooled pigment, and restrained detail create a minimalist folk-art quality with an atmospheric setting.
