# Weathered WWI Brodie Helmet

## Description

This reference centers on a World War I era Brodie helmet, characterized by its iconic wide-brimmed 'doughboy' silhouette and shallow bowl. The subject features a distinctive hand-painted camouflage pattern in muted forest green and deep navy blue, now heavily distressed with significant oxidation and surface pitting. The metallic texture shows authentic aging through rust spots and flaking paint, providing a tactile sense of historical weight. Its placement against a neutral interior frame highlights the helmet's profile and the specific mechanical detail of the top ventilation rivet.

## User context

A World War I Brodie helmet has a wide brim, shallow bowl, painted forest-green and navy camouflage, oxidation, pitting, rust, and flaking paint. Its neutral presentation highlights the silhouette and material aging.
