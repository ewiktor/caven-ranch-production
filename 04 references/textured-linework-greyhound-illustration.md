# Textured Linework Greyhound Illustration

## Description

This illustration utilizes a sophisticated mixed-media approach characterized by expressive, charcoal-like linework that varies in weight and pressure. The art direction focuses on a layered texture technique, where the top figure features a heavy, block-printed or stenciled pattern overlay that contrasts with the flatter, tonal fills of the lower figures. Shading is achieved through organic, gritty stippling and distressed edges rather than smooth gradients, giving the piece a tactile, hand-crafted quality. The detail level is intentionally selective, using minimal strokes to define the greyhounds' anatomy while allowing the paper-like grain of the background to integrate with the subjects.
