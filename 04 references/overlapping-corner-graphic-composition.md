# Overlapping Corner Graphic Composition

## Description

This layout demonstrates a dynamic graphic approach where a high-contrast blue vector illustration breaks the frame of a rectangular photograph, specifically overlapping the bottom-left corner. The technique creates a sense of depth and interaction between the 2D graphic and the 3D photographic space, bridging the white negative space of the canvas with the image content. This 'breaking the box' method is further echoed in the bottom layout with delicate white line-work that bleeds over the edge of the interior photography. The juxtaposition of the saturated cobalt blue against the natural desert tones emphasizes the graphic as a distinct, intentional layer.
