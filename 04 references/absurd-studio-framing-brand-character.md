# Absurd Studio Framing Brand Character

## Description

This reference establishes a unique brand character through an absurd, tight-crop composition that prioritizes the subject's ear and eye over a traditional portrait. The photographic style utilizes an 'old-timey' studio aesthetic with a muted, desaturated green backdrop and soft, diffused lighting that mimics vintage film stock. The intentional focus on specific anatomical features creates a sense of personality and curiosity, suggesting how a coyote character could be framed in unconventional, high-art studio contexts. The texture of the fur and the melancholic expression in the eye provide a blueprint for a consistent, slightly surreal brand mascot application.

## User context

An extreme crop prioritizes the animal’s ear and eye instead of showing a conventional portrait. A desaturated green background, diffused light, and vintage-film treatment create an unconventional but controlled character study.
