# High-Flash Trail Cam Character Aesthetic

## Description

This reference establishes a specific photographic treatment for a brand character, utilizing a harsh, direct high-flash technique that mimics the aesthetic of a trail camera or amateur snapshot. The lighting creates deep, high-contrast shadows and a flattened perspective, which should be applied to the coyote character to achieve an 'absurd' and raw brand presence. The composition uses a high-angle, slightly distorted perspective that makes the subject feel caught in a candid, unexpected moment. The texture of the subject's coat is hyper-emphasized by the flash, providing a blueprint for how to render the coyote's fur and features in various contexts.

## User context

Harsh direct flash creates deep shadows, flattened perspective, and a raw trail-camera quality. A high, slightly distorted viewpoint makes the animal feel caught in an unexpected moment, while the flash sharply emphasizes fur texture.
