# Minimalist Vertical Card Internal Layout

## Description

The internal layout of the cards utilizes a sophisticated vertical hierarchy with generous negative space to create a sense of luxury and breathing room. On the image-focused card, the photograph is cropped into a precise upper-third rectangle, balanced by a centered logo at the extreme bottom to anchor the composition. The text-heavy card employs a left-aligned typographic grid with varying font weights and sizes to establish a clear information architecture, ranging from large serif headlines to small-caps subheaders. Both cards maintain consistent internal margins that push the content inward, ensuring the layout feels contained and intentional within the card boundaries.
