# Vintage Boston Whaler Profile

## Description

A clean, side-profile view of a classic Boston Whaler skiff, emphasizing the iconic low-slung hull silhouette and distinctive blue interior liner. The composition provides a clear, unobstructed reference for a logo mark, focusing on the geometric relationship between the white hull, wood-trimmed center console, and the sharp horizontal plane of the water. The lighting is flat and even, preserving the essential shapes and lines necessary for conceptual brand development without visual clutter.
