# Historic Robinson Rosner Grocery Facade

## Description

This reference highlights the architectural graphics and historical subject matter of the 1891 Robinson Rosner building. The focus is on the ornate masonry and the arched 'Robinson Rosner' signage integrated into the pediment, showcasing late 19th-century commercial branding. The composition emphasizes the transition from the original brickwork and decorative cornices to the modern storefront, providing a clear study of how historical graphics persist on heritage structures. The black and white medium accentuates the textural contrast between the weathered brick, the smooth stone quoins, and the glass window panes.
