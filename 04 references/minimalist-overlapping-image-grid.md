# Minimalist Overlapping Image Grid

## Description

The layout utilizes a centered, minimalist composition within a sage green canvas, featuring two square images that overlap at their corners to create a dynamic focal point. A single line of sans-serif typography is positioned precisely above the image cluster, maintaining a significant amount of negative space to emphasize the central content. The arrangement relies on a tight, interlocking grid structure where the bottom-left image and top-right image share a vertical and horizontal axis, creating a sense of intentional asymmetry. This spatial relationship between the small-scale imagery and the expansive background color defines the sophisticated, editorial feel of the web frame.
