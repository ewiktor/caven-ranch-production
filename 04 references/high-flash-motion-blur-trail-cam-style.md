# High-Flash Motion Blur Trail Cam Style

## Description

This reference establishes a specific photographic treatment for a brand character, utilizing a high-intensity direct flash that mimics the raw, unpolished aesthetic of a trail camera. The subject is captured with significant motion blur, suggesting a candid and somewhat absurd 'caught in the act' moment that strips away traditional commercial polish. The flat, saturated blue background provides a stark, studio-like contrast to the chaotic movement, creating a unique tension between controlled environment and wild behavior. This stylistic approach should be applied to a coyote subject to create a recurring, slightly surreal brand presence across various contexts.

## User context

Direct flash and substantial motion blur create a raw, caught-in-the-act animal image. A flat blue backdrop contrasts a controlled studio environment with chaotic movement, producing a slightly surreal character treatment.
