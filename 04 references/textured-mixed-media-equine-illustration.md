# Textured Mixed-Media Equine Illustration

## Description

This illustration style utilizes a sophisticated layering of colored pencil and gouache-like textures to create a rich, tactile surface. The line work is delicate yet deliberate, defining the equine silhouettes with varying weights that suggest form without heavy outlines. Shading is achieved through dense, directional hatching and stippling, providing a high level of detail in the coat patterns and muscular anatomy. The art direction favors a flattened perspective with overlapping subjects, emphasizing the rhythmic repetition of shapes and the organic variation in texture across the composition.

## User context
