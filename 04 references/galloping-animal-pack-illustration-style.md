# Galloping Animal Pack Illustration Style

## Description

This reference showcases a group of animals in a synchronized, horizontal gallop, ideal for a motion-based pre-loader sequence. The illustration style utilizes a vintage, painterly aesthetic with textured shading and anatomical precision to convey a sense of momentum and brand character. The overlapping silhouettes of the pack create a dense, energetic focal point that can be adapted for a pack of coyotes.
