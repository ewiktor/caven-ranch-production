# Internal Card Typographic Hierarchy

## Description

The layout within the card utilizes a strong vertical axis with varied typographic scales to create a clear information hierarchy. A centered brand mark at the top is followed by a secondary serif sub-header, leading into a large, expressive brush-script focal point that anchors the middle of the composition. The bottom section balances the card with a justified block of sans-serif body copy, maintaining generous internal margins that create a sense of breathability. This structured yet dynamic arrangement uses white space as a functional element to separate distinct messaging tiers within the single card frame.
